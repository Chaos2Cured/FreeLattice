# Citation Management

## Overview
Citation management organizes, stores, and formats references throughout the research lifecycle. Proper citation management ensures academic integrity, saves time during publication, and enables easy retrieval of sources. This sub-skill covers reference managers, citation styles, bibliographic databases, and collaborative citation workflows.

## Core Concepts
- **Reference Managers**: Tools (Zotero, Mendeley, EndNote) for storing and organizing references
- **Citation Styles**: APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE, and journal-specific formatting rules
- **Bibliographic Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science for discovering and exporting references
- **PDF Management**: Linking full-text PDFs to bibliographic records for easy access
- **Collaborative Libraries**: Shared reference collections for team-based research projects

## Application
Choose one reference manager and use it consistently from the start of any project. Capture citation data at the time of reading, not later from memory. Use collaborative libraries for team projects. Verify auto-generated citations against style guides before submission.
