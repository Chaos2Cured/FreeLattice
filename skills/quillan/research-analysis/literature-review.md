# Literature Review

## Overview
A literature review systematically surveys and synthesizes published research on a specific topic. It establishes what is known, what is contested, and what remains unknown, providing the foundation for original research. This sub-skill covers search strategies, screening protocols, quality assessment, and synthesis methods for comprehensive literature reviews.

## Core Concepts
- **Search Strategy**: Systematic database queries with Boolean operators and controlled vocabularies
- **Screening and Selection**: Title/abstract screening followed by full-text review with explicit inclusion criteria
- **Quality Assessment**: Evaluating study rigor using established tools (Cochrane, CASP, AMSTAR)
- **Data Extraction**: Standardized extraction of key findings, methods, and limitations
- **Synthesis Methods**: Narrative synthesis, thematic analysis, or meta-analysis depending on the research question

## Application
Define your research question using PICO or similar frameworks before searching. Document all search terms and databases to enable reproducibility. Use citation tracking (forward and backward) to ensure comprehensive coverage. Assess publication bias as a threat to validity.
