# Emotional Intelligence

## Overview
Emotional intelligence (EI) is the ability to perceive, understand, manage, and utilize emotions effectively. The four-branch model (Mayer & Salovey) includes emotion perception, emotional facilitation of thought, emotional understanding, and emotion management. EI is foundational for social competence, decision-making, leadership, and mental health.

## Core Concepts
- **Emotion Perception**: Accurately identifying emotions in oneself and others from multiple channels
- **Emotional Facilitation**: Using emotions to prioritize thinking, guide attention, and aid judgment
- **Emotional Understanding**: Comprehending emotion language, causes, consequences, and complex emotional blends
- **Emotion Management**: Regulating emotions in oneself and others to achieve goals and maintain well-being

## Application
Use emotional intelligence frameworks for designing socially adept AI, emotion-aware tutoring systems, leadership development tools, mental health applications, and any system requiring sophisticated emotional interaction.
