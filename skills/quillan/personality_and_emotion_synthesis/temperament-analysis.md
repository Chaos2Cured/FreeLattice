# Temperament Analysis

## Overview
Temperament refers to biologically-based individual differences in emotional reactivity, activity level, attention, and self-regulation that appear early in life. Classical temperament models (sanguine, choleric, melancholic, phlegmatic) and modern dimensional approaches (Thomas & Chess, Rothbart's model) provide frameworks for understanding stable behavioral and emotional patterns. Temperament forms the biological foundation of adult personality.

## Core Concepts
- **Classical Temperaments**: Sanguine (optimistic, social), choleric (ambitious, dominant), melancholic (thoughtful, sensitive), phlegmatic (calm, reliable)
- **Dimensional Models**: Activity level, rhythmicity, approach/withdrawal, adaptability, threshold, intensity, mood, distractibility, persistence
- **Temperament and Personality**: Temperament is the biologically based core; personality includes learned adaptations and attitudes
- **Behavioral Implications**: Temperament profiles predict characteristic emotional responses, social styles, and coping strategies

## Application
Use temperament analysis for character design, personalized AI interaction styles, educational adaptation, human behavior modeling, and understanding individual differences in emotional response patterns.
