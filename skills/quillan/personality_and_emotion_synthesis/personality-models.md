# Personality Models

## Overview
Personality models provide theoretical frameworks describing the stable patterns of thought, emotion, and behavior that distinguish individuals. The Big Five (OCEAN: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism) is the most empirically validated model. HEXACO adds Honesty-Humility. Other models include MBTI (cognitive preferences) and temperament theory. These models enable systematic personality simulation.

## Core Concepts
- **Big Five (OCEAN)**: Five broad dimensions with facet-level sub-traits for detailed personality profiles
- **HEXACO Model**: Six dimensions including Honesty-Humility, important for ethical AI design
- **Trait Interactions**: How combinations of traits produce emergent behavioral patterns
- **Temporal Stability**: Personality traits are relatively stable but can change gradually over time

## Application
Use personality models for character creation, personalized AI behavior, adaptive user interfaces, game NPC design, and any system requiring consistent individual differences in behavior.
