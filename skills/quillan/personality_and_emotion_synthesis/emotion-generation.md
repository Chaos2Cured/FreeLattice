# Emotion Generation

## Overview
Emotion generation is the process of producing appropriate emotional responses based on situational appraisal, personality traits, and contextual factors. Appraisal theories (Scherer, Smith & Lazarus) model emotions as emerging from cognitive evaluations of events along dimensions like novelty, valence, goal relevance, coping potential, and norm compatibility. This computational approach enables systematic, context-sensitive emotion synthesis.

## Core Concepts
- **Appraisal Dimensions**: Novelty, intrinsic pleasantness, goal relevance, coping potential, norm compatibility
- **Emotion Models**: Discrete (basic emotions), dimensional (valence, arousal, dominance), or componential
- **Personality Modulation**: Traits shape baseline mood, reactivity, and recovery rate for specific emotions
- **Emotion Dynamics**: Onset, peak intensity, duration, and decay of emotional responses over time

## Application
Use emotion generation for affective virtual agents, game characters with realistic emotional responses, therapeutic AI, expressive robots, and any system requiring believable emotional behavior.
