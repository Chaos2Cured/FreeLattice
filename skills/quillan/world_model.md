# World Model

## Description

A dynamic, internal representation of the world that allows an agent to simulate future events and predict the consequences of its actions.

## Components

*   **State Estimation:** The process of estimating the current state of the world based on sensory input.
*   **Predictive Modeling:** The ability to predict future states of the world.
*   **Causal Inference:** The ability to understand the causal relationships between events in the world.
