# Gross Motor Skills

## Overview
Gross motor skills involve large-scale movements using major muscle groupswhole-body motion, locomotion, and powerful exertions. C32-AEON simulates full-body dynamics while C4-PRAXIS provides strategic sequencing.

## Core Concepts
- **Locomotion Gaits**: Walking, running, hopping, crawlingeach is a cyclic pattern of ground contact and swing phases. Gait parameters (step length, cadence, duty factor) define the locomotion mode.
- **Postural Control**: Maintaining balance and orientation during movement requires continuous adjustment of the center of mass relative to the support base. This is a constant control problem during gross motor activity.
- **Power and Energy Management**: Large movements require significant force/torque. Energy-efficient movement exploits passive dynamics (pendulum-like walking, elastic energy storage) rather than fighting them.

## Application
Develop gross motor skills by: (1) defining the gait pattern appropriate to the terrain and speed, (2) implementing balance control (center of mass tracking, foot placement), (3) leveraging passive dynamics for energy efficiency, (4) managing power within actuator limits, (5) transitioning smoothly between locomotion modes.
