# Fine Motor Skills

## Overview
Fine motor skills involve precise, small-scale movements requiring accuracy and dexterity. C10-CODEWEAVER contributes algorithmic precision while C20-ARTIFEX provides tool integration for delicate operations.

## Core Concepts
- **Precision vs Speed Trade-off**: Higher precision typically requires slower movement (Fitts' Law). The index of difficulty (ID = log2(2A/W)) quantifies the precision requirement based on amplitude and target width.
- **Compliance and Force Control**: Fine manipulation requires sensing and controlling contact forces, not just positions. Impedance and admittance control enable precise force application.
- **Tremor and Stability**: Small involuntary movements (tremor) limit precision. Filtering (low-pass, notch), mechanical damping, and stiffening strategies reduce tremor effects at the cost of speed.

## Application
Develop fine motor skills by: (1) analyzing the precision requirements using Fitts' Law, (2) implementing force/position hybrid control for contact tasks, (3) applying appropriate filtering for stability, (4) practicing with graduated difficulty (larger targets first, then smaller), (5) measuring accuracy and precision quantitatively.
