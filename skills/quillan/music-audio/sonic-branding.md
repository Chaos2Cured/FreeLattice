# Sonic Branding

## Overview
Sonic branding creates memorable audio identities that represent brands, products, or experiences. In an increasingly audio-driven world, sonic identity is as important as visual identity for recognition and emotional connection. This sub-skill covers audio logo design, brand soundscapes, psychoacoustic optimization, and multi-touchpoint consistency.

## Core Concepts
- **Audio Logo Design**: Creating short, memorable sound marks (2-5 seconds)
- **Brand Soundscape**: The complete sonic palette representing a brand across touchpoints
- **Mnemonic Midrange**: The 300 Hz to 2 kHz range where brand sounds are most recognizable
- **Psychoacoustic Optimization**: Designing sounds that cut through auditory clutter
- **Cross-Touchpoint Consistency**: Maintaining sonic coherence across TV, radio, digital, and physical spaces

## Application
Design for the mnemonic midrange where human hearing is most sensitive. Test audio logos at low volumes and in noisy environments. A sonic brand should work across all touchpoints without modification. The best audio logos are simple enough to hum but distinctive enough to identify in seconds.
