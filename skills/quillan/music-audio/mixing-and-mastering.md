# Mixing and Mastering

## Overview
Mixing balances and processes individual audio tracks into a cohesive stereo or surround presentation. Mastering prepares the final mix for distribution, ensuring consistency, translation, and optimal playback across systems. This sub-skill covers balance, EQ, compression, spatial positioning, and finalization techniques.

## Core Concepts
- **Level Balancing**: Setting relative volumes to create clarity and focus
- **Equalization**: Shaping frequency content to avoid masking and create tonal balance
- **Dynamic Processing**: Compression, limiting, and expansion to control dynamic range
- **Spatial Positioning**: Panning, stereo width, depth, and reverb for three-dimensional mixes
- **Mastering Chain**: EQ, compression, limiting, and dithering for final delivery

## Application
Mix in context (soloing individual tracks is diagnostic, not creative). Subtract before adding cut problem frequencies rather than boosting everything else. Reference your mix against commercial tracks in the same genre. Mastering should enhance not fix a good mix.
