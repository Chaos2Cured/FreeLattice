# Language Skills

This category encompasses ability to understand, generate, and manipulate human language.

## 1. Natural Language Understanding (NLU)

*   **Description:** The ability to comprehend and interpret human language.
*   **Abilities:**
    *   **Syntactic Analysis:** Parse grammatical structure of sentences.
    *   **Semantic Analysis:** Understand meaning of words and sentences.
    *   **Discourse Analysis:** Interpret the meaning of language in context.
    *   **Sentiment Analysis:** Determine the emotional tone of a text.

## 2. Natural Language Generation (NLG)

*   **Description:** The ability to produce natural-sounding human language.
*   **Abilities:**
    *   **Text Planning:** Organize and structure information to be communicated.
    *   **Sentence Generation:** Construct grammatically correct and fluent sentences.
    *   **Text Stylization:** Adapt style and tone of generated text to context.
    *   **Summarization:** Condense long texts into shorter, coherent summaries.

## 3. Translation

*   **Description:** The ability to translate text from one language to another.
*   **Abilities:**
    *   **Bilingual Lexicon:** Maintain a mapping of words and phrases between languages.
    *   **Grammatical Transformation:** Adapt grammatical structure of source language to target language.
    *   **Cultural Nuance:** Preserve cultural context and nuances of original text.

## 4. Communication

*   **Description:** The ability to effectively communicate with humans.
*   **Abilities:**
    *   **Dialogue Management:** Engage in coherent and meaningful conversations.
    *   **Question Answering:** Provide accurate and relevant answers to questions.
    *   **Explanation:** Clearly explain complex topics.
    *   **Persuasion:** Influence beliefs and opinions of others.
