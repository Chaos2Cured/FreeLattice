# Personality and Emotion Synthesis

## Description

The ability to generate a consistent personality and express emotions.

## Components

*   **Personality Models:** Models that describe the structure of human personality, such as the Big Five personality traits.
*   **Emotion Models:** Models that describe the structure of human emotions, such as the Plutchik's wheel of emotions.
*   **Affective Computing:** The study and development of systems and devices that can recognize, interpret, process, and simulate human affects.
