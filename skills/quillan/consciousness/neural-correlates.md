# Neural Correlates of Consciousness
## Overview
The neural correlates of consciousness are the minimal set of neural events and structures jointly sufficient for a specific conscious experience. This sub-skill surveys empirical findings on brain activity patterns associated with conscious awareness.
## Core Concepts
- **Minimal Sufficiency**: The smallest neural system whose activity is sufficient for a given conscious experience
- **Recurrent Processing**: Feedback loops between higher and lower cortical areas necessary for conscious perception
- **Neural Synchrony**: Temporal coordination of neural firing across distributed brain regions
- **Fronto-Parietal Network**: Core neural substrate supporting global availability and conscious access
## Application
Apply this when grounding consciousness theories in empirical neuroscience, designing brain-inspired architectures, or evaluating claims about consciousness in artificial and biological systems.
