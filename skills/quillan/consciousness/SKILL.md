# Consciousness
A comprehensive skill for understanding and applying consciousness concepts including subjective experience, phenomenal awareness, and access consciousness. Provides frameworks for modeling conscious-like awareness and designing systems with self-reflection capabilities.
## Sub-Skills
1. **qualia-theory.md** -- Subjective, first-person character of experience
2. **self-awareness.md** -- Reflexive and metacognitive aspects of consciousness
3. **phenomenal-consciousness.md** -- Raw felt character of sensations and emotions
4. **access-consciousness.md** -- Global availability for reasoning and report
5. **neural-correlates.md** -- Neural events associated with conscious states
6. **conscious-vs-unconscious.md** -- Boundaries and transitions in awareness
7. **artificial-consciousness.md** -- Computational approaches to machine consciousness
