# Access Consciousness
## Overview
Access consciousness concerns the availability of information for reasoning, speech, and behavioral control -- the functional dimension of consciousness. A mental state is access-conscious when its content is poised for global access by cognitive systems including working memory, evaluation, and verbal report.
## Core Concepts
- **Global Workspace Theory**: Information becomes conscious when it gains access to a global workspace available to multiple consumer systems
- **Information Poisedness**: Content is conscious when it is ready for direct control of thought and action
- **Dissociability**: Conditions where phenomenal and access consciousness come apart such as blindsight and neglect
- **Reportability**: The ability to verbally or behaviorally report on conscious content
## Application
Use this when designing systems that distinguish between globally available information and merely processed information, or when analyzing cases where phenomenal experience and functional access diverge.
