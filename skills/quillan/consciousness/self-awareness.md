# Self-Awareness
## Overview
Self-awareness within consciousness studies refers to the capacity to become the object of ones own attention -- the reflexive nature of conscious experience where the subject is aware of itself as a subject. This sub-skill covers metacognitive awareness, self-consciousness, and the recursive structure of introspective access.
## Core Concepts
- **Reflexive Awareness**: The ability of consciousness to take itself as its own object of attention
- **Metacognitive Access**: Higher-order representations of ones own cognitive and perceptual states
- **Self-as-Subject vs Self-as-Object**: The distinction between the self that experiences and the self that is experienced
- **Mirror Self-Recognition**: Empirical markers of self-awareness in developmental and comparative psychology
## Application
Use this when designing systems with introspective capabilities, modeling recursive self-monitoring architectures, or analyzing the phenomenology of self-consciousness.
