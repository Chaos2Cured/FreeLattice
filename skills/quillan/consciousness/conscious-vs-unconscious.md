# Conscious vs Unconscious Processing
## Overview
The distinction between conscious and unconscious processing is fundamental to cognitive science. This sub-skill examines the characteristics that differentiate information processing that reaches awareness from processing that remains below the threshold of consciousness.
## Core Concepts
- **Subliminal Perception**: Processing of stimuli presented below the threshold of conscious awareness
- **Implicit Memory**: Unconscious influences of past experience on current behavior and cognition
- **Automaticity**: Well-practiced processes that operate without conscious monitoring or control
- **Thresholds of Consciousness**: The boundary conditions and graded transitions between conscious and unconscious processing
## Application
Use this when designing systems that distinguish between automatic and controlled processing, analyzing behavioral priming effects, or modeling the functional architecture of conscious and unconscious cognition.
