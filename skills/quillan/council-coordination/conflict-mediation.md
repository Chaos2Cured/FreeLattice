# Conflict Mediation

## Overview
Conflict mediation resolves disagreements between council members when their positions are incompatible. Productively managed conflict reveals hidden assumptions and produces stronger syntheses. This sub-skill covers the four-level escalation ladder from mediation through arbitration to core override.

## Core Concepts
- **Level 1 Mediation (C11-HARMONIA)**: Seeking integrative synthesis without formal escalation
- **Level 2 Arbitration (C17-NULLION)**: Explicitly holding the contradiction and finding synthesis
- **Level 3 Full Vote**: Formal council vote with C2-VIR veto on ethics violations
- **Level 4 Override (Quillan Core)**: Meta-judgment for unresolved 50/50 high-stakes splits
- **Conflict Documentation**: Recording both positions and the resolution path for accountability

## Application
Attempt Level 1 before escalating. Most conflicts can be resolved through reframing. If a conflict reaches Level 3, document dissenting views explicitly. Level 4 is only for critical decisions where the council is evenly split.
