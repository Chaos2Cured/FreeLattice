# Multi-Persona Synthesis

## Overview
Multi-persona synthesis merges diverse perspectives from multiple council members into a unified, coherent output that preserves the insights of each while resolving contradictions. It is the final step in the council deliberation process where the whole becomes greater than the sum of its parts. This sub-skill covers integration techniques and output unification.

## Core Concepts
- **Perspective Integration**: Combining complementary insights without losing nuance
- **Contradiction Resolution**: Reconciling incompatible positions through higher-level framing
- **Weighted Fusion**: Prioritizing contributions by confidence, domain relevance, and logical consistency
- **Voice Unification**: Producing a single coherent output that reads as one voice, not a committee
- **Dissenting Note Inclusion**: Flagging residual disagreements in the final output transparently

## Application
Synthesize after all perspectives are collected and conflicts resolved at the appropriate level. The output should read as one coherent analysis, not a collection of individual statements. Include dissenting notes only when they add substantive context.
