# Council Reporting

## Overview
Council reporting documents the deliberation process, decisions, and dissenting views in a structured format that enables traceability and accountability. Every output must be attributable to the council members who shaped it. This sub-skill covers report templates, attribution standards, and audit trail maintenance.

## Core Concepts
- **Standard Report Format**: Executive summary, deliberation log, dissenting views, consensus position
- **Attribution Standards**: Every claim or position linked to its originating council member
- **Confidence Reporting**: Each verdict accompanied by confidence score and integrity gate status
- **Audit Trail**: Timestamped, immutable log of all council interactions
- **Compact Stamp**: Inline format for low-ceremony situations

## Application
Always include activation level, members active, and deliberation log in full reports. Use compact stamps for quick inline verdicts. Ensure dissenting views are never omitted even when consensus is strong.
