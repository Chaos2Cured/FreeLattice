# Decision Making
## Overview
Decision making is the cognitive process of selecting a course of action from among multiple alternatives. This sub-skill covers normative, descriptive, and prescriptive decision theories, including multi-criteria analysis, risk assessment, and group decision processes.
## Core Concepts
- **Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis**: Evaluating alternatives across multiple weighted criteria using decision matrices
- **Expected Value**: Calculating the probability-weighted average of possible outcomes
- **Bounded Rationality**: The recognition that real decision makers operate under cognitive and informational constraints
- **Group Decision Making**: Techniques for reaching consensus including Delphi method and nominal group technique
## Application
Apply decision-making frameworks when choosing between competing solutions, making strategic choices under uncertainty, or designing automated decision systems.
