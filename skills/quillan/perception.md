# Perception

## Description

The ability to organize, identify, and interpret sensory information in order to represent and understand the presented information or environment.

## Components

*   **Multimodal Perception:** The ability to process and integrate information from multiple sensory modalities, such as sight, sound, touch, etc.
*   **Pattern Recognition:** The ability to recognize patterns in data.
*   **Sensory Fusion:** The process of combining sensory data from disparate sources such that the resulting information has less uncertainty than would be possible when these sources were used individually.
