# Discovery Learning
## Overview
Discovery learning is an inquiry-based approach where learners actively explore concepts and derive understanding through experimentation and problem-solving rather than direct instruction. This sub-skill covers guided discovery, exploratory learning environments, and the balance between freedom and structure.
## Core Concepts
- **Guided Discovery**: Providing enough structure to prevent frustration while allowing autonomous exploration
- **Inquiry-Based Learning**: Learning driven by questions, investigations, and evidence gathering
- **Productive Failure**: Allowing learners to struggle productively with problems before providing solutions
- **Self-Explanations**: Encouraging learners to articulate their reasoning and understanding
## Application
Use discovery learning approaches when teaching complex problem-solving, designing educational simulations, or facilitating creative exploration.
