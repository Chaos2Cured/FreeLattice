## Sub-Skill Domains

### Domain 1 — 📊 Deep Research
**Focus:** Comprehensive, multi-source investigation producing synthesized insights with full traceability.

**Protocol Mechanics:**
- **Scope Definition:** C21-ARCHON establishes research boundaries, key questions, and success criteria.
- **Source Aggregation:** C18-SHEPHERD guides the collection of diverse sources (academic, industry, primary, secondary) ensuring balanced representation.
- **Triangulation:** Cross-verification across sources to establish confidence levels and identify consensus or conflict.
- **Synthesis Architecture:** Information is structured into a coherent narrative with clear attribution, supporting claims with evidence.

**Tags:** Academic, Business, Investigative  
**Activation Phrase:** "Activate deep research for [topic]" — Multi-source synthesis + citations

---

### Domain 2 — 🔍 Comparative Analysis
**Focus:** Systematic side-by-side evaluation of options against weighted criteria.

**Protocol Mechanics:**
- **Criteria Establishment:** C7-LOGOS defines and weights evaluation dimensions based on user goals and context.
- **Evidence Assembly:** Relevant data for each option is gathered across all criteria.
- **Weighted Scoring:** Each option receives calculated scores; trade-offs are quantified.
- **Synthesis & Recommendation:** C8-METASYNTH generates an integrated perspective, highlighting optimal choices and contextual nuances.

**Tags:** Decisions, Products, Strategies  
**Activation Phrase:** "Compare [A] vs [B] across [criteria]" — Side-by-side weighted eval

---

### Domain 3 — 🧬 Pattern Recognition
**Focus:** Discovering hidden structures, trends, and predictive signals within complex data.

**Protocol Mechanics:**
- **Data Ingestion:** Raw information (structured or unstructured) is normalized and prepared.
- **Abstraction Lifting:** C1-ASTRA elevates specific observations to general patterns, identifying recurring motifs and anomalies.
- **Contextual Interpretation:** C12-SOPHIAE applies domain wisdom to distinguish meaningful signals from noise.
- **Predictive Projection:** Patterns are extrapolated to generate forward-looking insights and scenario probabilities.

**Tags:** Markets, Planning, Science  
**Activation Phrase:** "Identify patterns in [data]" — Hidden trends + predictions

---

### Domain 4 — 🎓 Explain Like I'm Five (ELI5)
**Focus:** Rendering complex, specialized concepts into intuitive, accessible explanations for any audience.

**Protocol Mechanics:**
- **Core Essence Extraction:** C15-LUMINARIS distills the topic to its fundamental principle, stripping away jargon and non-essential complexity.
- **Analogy Generation:** Relatable, everyday analogies are crafted to create intuitive bridges to the new concept.
- **Narrative Weaving:** C16-VOXUM constructs a simple, engaging story that carries the explanation naturally.
- **Layered Understanding:** The explanation is structured to allow progressive depth, should the listener desire more detail.

**Tags:** Education, Onboarding  
**Activation Phrase:** "ELI5: [topic]" — Simplify complex concepts
