# Scenario Generation

## Overview
Scenario generation creates diverse, plausible futures to support robust planning and decision-making under uncertainty. Instead of predicting a single outcome, it explores a range of possibilities each consistent with current knowledge. This sub-skill covers exploratory scenarios, stress tests, and normative scenario design.

## Core Concepts
- **Exploratory Scenarios**: What could happen under different driving forces and uncertainties
- **Normative Scenarios**: How to achieve a desired future state working backward
- **Stress Testing**: Testing plans against extreme but plausible scenarios
- **Signpost Identification**: Events or indicators that suggest which scenario is unfolding
- **Scenario Diversity**: Ensuring scenarios span the range of plausible outcomes, not just the median

## Application
Generate 3-5 scenarios that span the range of plausible futures include best case, worst case, and one or two middle paths. For each, identify signposts that would indicate it is unfolding. The goal is not to predict the future but to be prepared for multiple futures.
