# Hierarchical Planning

## Overview
Hierarchical planning creates plans at multiple levels of abstraction, from strategic direction to operational execution. Each level provides the right granularity for its stakeholders while maintaining traceability to higher-level objectives. This sub-skill covers top-down refinement, bottom-up constraint propagation, and Hierarchical Task Networks (HTNs).

## Core Concepts
- **Strategic Level**: Long-term direction, major milestones, and resource commitments
- **Tactical Level**: Phase objectives, major deliverables, and intermediate deadlines
- **Operational Level**: Executable actions with concrete inputs, outputs, and acceptance criteria
- **Top-Down Refinement**: Decomposing high-level tasks into lower-level methods
- **Bottom-Up Propagation**: Aggregating constraints and estimates upward through the hierarchy

## Application
Define 3-4 levels of abstraction maximum. Each level should be complete at its own granularity. Maintain bidirectional traceability: every operational task should link to a tactical objective, which links to a strategic goal. Use HTN formalism for complex, recurring planning domains.
