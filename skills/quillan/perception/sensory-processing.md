# Sensory Processing
## Overview
Sensory processing is the mechanism by which sensory organs and the nervous system receive, transduce, and transmit signals from the environment. This sub-skill covers the biological and computational foundations of sensing, including transduction principles, neural encoding, and early-stage processing across modalities.
## Core Concepts
- **Transduction**: Conversion of physical energy (light, sound, pressure) into neural signals
- **Receptive Fields**: The region of sensory space that modulates a neurons firing rate
- **Lateral Inhibition**: The phenomenon where excited neurons inhibit neighboring neurons, enhancing contrast
- **Sensory Adaptation**: Decreased sensitivity to constant stimulation over time
## Application
Apply this when designing sensor processing pipelines, understanding the biological foundations of perception, or building bio-inspired sensing systems.
