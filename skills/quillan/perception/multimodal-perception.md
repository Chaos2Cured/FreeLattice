# Multimodal Perception
## Overview
Multimodal perception is the integration of information from multiple sensory modalities into a unified perceptual experience. This sub-skill covers cross-modal correspondences, sensory dominance, the McGurk effect, and architectures for fusing heterogeneous sensory data.
## Core Concepts
- **Cross-Modal Integration**: Combining information from different senses to form a coherent percept
- **Sensory Dominance**: The tendency for one modality (typically vision) to dominate perception when modalities conflict
- **Cross-Modal Correspondences**: Natural associations between features across modalities
- **Temporal Synchrony**: The alignment of signals across modalities in time critical for binding
## Application
Apply this when designing multimodal AI systems, sensor fusion pipelines for autonomous vehicles, or human-computer interfaces that leverage multiple sensory channels.
