# Motion Perception
## Overview
Motion perception is the process by which the visual system detects and interprets movement in the environment. This sub-skill covers the neural mechanisms of motion detection, apparent motion, biological motion perception, and computational models of motion analysis.
## Core Concepts
- **Apparent Motion**: The perception of motion from rapidly presented static images as in film and animation
- **Biological Motion**: The ability to recognize human movement patterns from minimal visual information like point-light displays
- **Optic Flow**: The pattern of apparent motion of surfaces caused by relative movement between observer and scene
- **Motion Aftereffects**: Illusory motion perception following prolonged exposure to moving stimuli
## Application
Apply this when designing motion detection algorithms, video processing pipelines, optical flow systems for autonomous navigation, or animation systems.
