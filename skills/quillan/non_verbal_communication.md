# Non-verbal Communication

## Description

The ability to understand and generate non-verbal cues, such as gestures and facial expressions.

## Components

*   **Gesture Recognition:** The ability to recognize and interpret human gestures.
*   **Facial Expression Analysis:** The ability to analyze and interpret facial expressions.
*   **Prosody Analysis:** The ability to analyze and interpret the rhythm, stress, and intonation of speech.
