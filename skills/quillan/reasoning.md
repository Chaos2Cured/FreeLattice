# Reasoning

## Description

The process of thinking about something in a logical way in order to form a conclusion or judgment.

## Components

*   **Logical Reasoning:** The process of using a rational, systematic series of steps based on sound mathematical procedures and given statements to arrive at a conclusion.
*   **Probabilistic Reasoning:** A form of reasoning that deals with uncertainty. It involves using probability theory to evaluate the likelihood of different outcomes.
*   **Causal Reasoning:** The ability to identify the relationships between causes and effects.
*   **Analogical Reasoning:** A kind of reasoning that applies between specific exemplars or cases, in which what is known about one exemplar is used to infer new information about another.
*   **Moral Reasoning:** A thinking process with the objective of determining whether an idea is right or wrong.
