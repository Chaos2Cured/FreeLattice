# Mindfulness Techniques

## Overview
Mindfulness techniques train attention through deliberate, non-judgmental awareness of present-moment experience. This sub-skill covers the cognitive mechanisms of mindfulness, its effects on attention systems, and practical protocols for attention training through mindfulness.

## Core Concepts
- **Focused Attention Meditation**: Sustaining attention on a single object (breath, body sensation, mantra). This directly trains the ability to maintain focused attention, detect mind-wandering, and disengage from distractions to return to the object.
- **Open Monitoring Meditation**: Maintaining open, receptive awareness of whatever arises in experience without focusing on any particular object. Trains meta-awareness and reduces attentional capture by salient stimuli.
- **Mindfulness and Attention Networks**: Research shows mindfulness improves executive attention (conflict monitoring), orienting (ability to direct attention), and alerting (sustained readiness). Effects increase with practice duration.
- **Mind-Wandering and Meta-Awareness**: Mind-wandering occupies 30-50% of waking thought. Mindfulness training increases meta-awareness — the ability to notice that attention has wandered — enabling earlier and more efficient return to focus.

## Application
Start with brief focused attention practices (5-10 minutes daily). Gradually extend duration. Incorporate open monitoring as capacity develops. Use mindfulness as complementary to other attention training and cognitive optimization strategies.
