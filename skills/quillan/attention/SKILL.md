# Attention

The cognitive process of selectively concentrating on specific aspects of the environment while filtering out others. Covers both human cognitive attention mechanisms and computational attention systems — providing protocols for focus management, multi-tasking, salient signal detection, and resource allocation across biological and artificial processing systems.

## Sub-Skills
1. Focused Attention — Concentrating resources on a single task or stimulus
2. Sustained Attention — Maintaining focus over extended periods
3. Selective Attention — Filtering relevant from irrelevant information
4. Divided Attention — Managing multiple simultaneous demands
5. Attention Control — Executive capacity to direct and shift attention
6. Mindfulness Techniques — Present-moment attention training
7. Attention Training — Systematic protocols for improving attentional capacity
