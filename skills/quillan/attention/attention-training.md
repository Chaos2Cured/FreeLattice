# Attention Training

## Overview
Attention training encompasses systematic protocols for improving attentional capacity, control, and resilience through structured practice and cognitive exercises. This sub-skill covers evidence-based training approaches, computerized cognitive training, and transfer of training effects.

## Core Concepts
- **Cognitive Training Paradigms**: Adaptive dual n-back training (working memory and attention control), task-switching training (cognitive flexibility), Stroop-like training (inhibitory control), and attention network training.
- **Transfer Effects**: Near transfer (improvement on trained task and similar tasks) is reliable. Far transfer (improvement on unrelated cognitive abilities) is limited. Training effects are strongest for the specific trained processes.
- **Video Game Training**: Action video game training improves visual attention, spatial distribution of attention, and multiple-object tracking. Improvements are largest for attentional speed and control.
- **Training Principles**: Adaptivity (difficulty adjusts to performance), variety (multiple training tasks), engagement (sustained interest), and dosage (sufficient practice hours). Brief training (< 10 hours) produces modest, often non-lasting effects.

## Application
Choose training protocols that target specific attentional weaknesses. Combine multiple approaches for broader effects. Maintain training for sufficient duration (20+ hours for lasting change). Use real-world transfer tasks to reinforce training gains.
