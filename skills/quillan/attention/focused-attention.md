# Focused Attention

## Overview
Focused attention is the ability to concentrate mental resources on a single task or stimulus while ignoring distractions. This sub-skill covers the mechanisms of concentration, the psychology of flow states, and practical techniques for deep focus in distraction-rich environments.

## Core Concepts
- **Concentration Mechanisms**: Selective enhancement of task-relevant neural representations coupled with active inhibition of distractors. Prefrontal cortex orchestrates top-down attention control.
- **Flow State**: Deep immersion where self-awareness diminishes, time perception distorts, and performance peaks. Flow requires a balance between task challenge and skill level, clear goals, and immediate feedback.
- **Attention Restoration**: Directed attention fatigues with use and recovers through rest, especially in natural environments (Attention Restoration Theory). Brief breaks, nature exposure, and sleep restore attentional capacity.
- **Deep Work Protocols**: Time-blocking, environment design (removing distractions), single-tasking commitments, and structured recovery periods. Consistent deep work practice increases attentional stamina.

## Application
Structure work sessions with clear goals, appropriate challenge levels, and minimized distractions. Build attentional stamina gradually. Schedule recovery periods. Use environmental design to reduce the willpower needed for sustained focus.
