# Summarization

## Overview
Summarization condenses longer texts into shorter versions while preserving the most important information and overall meaning. Extractive summarization selects and combines existing sentences from the source; abstractive summarization generates novel sentences expressing the source's key content. Both approaches require content selection, information compression, and coherence maintenance.

## Core Concepts
- **Extractive Summarization**: Scoring and selecting salient sentences; redundancy removal; ordering
- **Abstractive Summarization**: Content planning, fusion, paraphrasing, and novel sentence generation
- **Single vs. Multi-Document**: Summarizing one source vs. synthesizing information across multiple sources
- **Faithfulness and Factuality**: Ensuring generated summaries contain only information present in sources

## Application
Use summarization for document abstraction, news aggregation, meeting minutes, literature review synthesis, and any task requiring concise representation of longer content.
