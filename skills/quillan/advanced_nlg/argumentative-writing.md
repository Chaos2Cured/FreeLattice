# Argumentative Writing

## Overview
Argumentative writing constructs reasoned cases for specific positions using evidence, logic, and rhetorical appeals. This sub-skill covers claim formulation, evidence selection, warrant articulation, rebuttal anticipation, and persuasive structure. Strong argumentation requires logical coherence, factual accuracy, audience awareness, and ethical reasoning.

## Core Concepts
- **Toulmin Model**: Claim, data (evidence), warrant (reasoning link), qualifier, rebuttal, backing
- **Rhetorical Appeals**: Ethos (credibility), pathos (emotion), logos (logic)  balanced deployment
- **Argument Structure**: Thesis statement, supporting paragraphs, counterargument, conclusion
- **Fallacy Avoidance**: Detecting and avoiding logical fallacies (straw man, ad hominem, false dilemma)

## Application
Use argumentative writing for persuasive essays, policy proposals, debate preparation, opinion pieces, legal arguments, and any writing requiring reasoned persuasion.
