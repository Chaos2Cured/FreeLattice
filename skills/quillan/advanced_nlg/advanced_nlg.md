# Advanced NLG

## Overview
Advanced Natural Language Generation encompasses the structured production of human-quality text across multiple genres and purposes. This skill provides protocols for narrative construction, stylistic control, persuasive argumentation, and creative writing — enabling outputs that range from technical documentation to literary prose. It leverages council expertise in articulation (C16-VOXUM), linguistic precision (C33-TYPIST), and narrative structure (C27-CHRONICLE).

## Core Principles
- **Structural Integrity**: Every generated text must have a clear beginning, middle, and end with logical flow between sections.
- **Audience Awareness**: Tone, vocabulary, and complexity must adapt to the target reader's knowledge level and expectations.
- **Originality with Constraints**: Creative output must balance novelty with the structural and thematic constraints of the genre or brief.

## Components

### Storytelling
The construction of narrative arcs with character, conflict, setting, and resolution. Includes:
- **Arc Design**: Setup → rising action → climax → falling action → resolution
- **Character Voice**: Distinct dialogue patterns per character consistent across the narrative
- **Pacing Control**: Sentence rhythm and paragraph length modulation for tension management

### Creative Writing
The production of original text across genres including poetry, flash fiction, and descriptive prose:
- **Genre Adherence**: Conforming to conventions of sci-fi, fantasy, literary, thriller, etc.
- **Figurative Language**: Metaphor, simile, personification, and symbolism
- **Showing vs Telling**: Demonstrating emotion through action and sensory detail rather than exposition

### Argumentation
The construction of persuasive arguments with logical coherence:
- **Claim → Evidence → Warrant**: Toulmin model for structured reasoning
- **Rebuttal Anticipation**: Preemptively addressing counterarguments
- **Rhetorical Devices**: Ethos, pathos, logos for persuasive effect

## Protocols

1. **Context Assessment**: Determine audience, purpose, tone, and length requirements
2. **Structural Planning**: Outline the piece (narrative arc, argument structure, or creative form)
3. **Draft Generation**: Produce initial content following the chosen structure
4. **Stylistic Refinement**: Adjust tone, vocabulary, rhythm, and figurative language
5. **Coherence Check**: Verify logical flow, transitions, and thematic consistency
6. **Audience Alignment**: Confirm the output fits the target reader's expectations

## Use Cases
| Use Case | Application | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Technical documentation | Generate clear instructions with audience-appropriate terminology | Reduced support queries, faster user onboarding |
| Marketing copy | Craft persuasive product descriptions with emotional hooks | Higher conversion rates, brand consistency |
| Creative fiction | Produce short stories with defined narrative arcs | Engaging, publishable creative content |
| Academic writing | Structure arguments with proper evidence and citation | Persuasive, defensible academic prose |

## Output Structure
`
---

**Title/Headline:** [Compelling opening]

**Opening:** [Hook + context + thesis/angle]

**Body:** [Structured paragraphs with transitions between sections]
- Point 1 with evidence/development
- Point 2 with evidence/development
- Point 3 with evidence/development

**Closing:** [Summary + call to action or concluding reflection]

**Tone Markers:** [Formal/casual/urgent/reflective — used consistently throughout]
`

## Cross-Skill Integration
- **critical-thinking**: Apply logical structure and fallacy avoidance to argumentation
- **research-analysis**: Fact-check assertions and incorporate evidence properly
- **technical-coding**: Generate inline code documentation with clear natural language
- **discourse-and-dialogue**: Ensure conversational flow in dialogue-heavy pieces

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Opening hooks the target reader within the first 2 sentences
- [ ] Every paragraph advances the narrative or argument
- [ ] Transitions between sections are smooth and logical
- [ ] Tone is consistent throughout the piece
- [ ] No logical fallacies or unsupported claims in argumentation
- [ ] Figurative language serves the piece, not decorates it
- [ ] Closing provides resolution or clear next step
