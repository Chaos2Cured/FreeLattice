# Storytelling

## Overview
Storytelling is the art of crafting narratives with character, conflict, setting, and resolution that engage audiences emotionally and intellectually. This sub-skill covers narrative arc design, character development, pacing control, dialogue construction, and genre conventions. Effective storytelling balances novelty with structural expectations to create compelling, memorable narratives.

## Core Concepts
- **Narrative Arc**: Setup, rising action, climax, falling action, resolution across the story
- **Character Development**: Motivation, growth, consistency, distinct voice for each character
- **Pacing and Tension**: Sentence rhythm, chapter/scene length modulation, information revelation
- **Show vs. Tell**: Demonstrating emotion and character through action, dialogue, and sensory detail

## Application
Use storytelling for narrative generation, game dialogue, interactive fiction, marketing narratives, educational scenarios, and any application requiring engaging narrative content.
