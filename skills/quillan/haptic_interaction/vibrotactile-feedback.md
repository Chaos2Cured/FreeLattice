# Vibrotactile Feedback

## Overview
Vibrotactile feedback uses vibrations to convey information through the skin. It is the most widely deployed haptic technology due to its simplicity and low cost. C23-CADENCE provides rhythmic precision for tactile patterns.

## Core Concepts
- **Vibration Actuators**: Eccentric rotating mass (ERM) motorssimple but slow response. Linear resonant actuators (LRA)efficient at specific frequencies. Piezoelectric actuatorsbroadband, fast, precise.
- **Tactile Illusions**: Apparent haptic motion (vibrating two points sequentially creates the sensation of continuous motion), sensory saltation (rapid sequential taps appear to jump), phantom sensation (varying amplitudes creates perceived intermediate points).
- **Encoding Information**: Frequency, amplitude, duration, rhythm, and body location all encode information. Pattern design is critical: learnable patterns (rhythms, spatial trajectories) convey more than simple alerts.

## Application
Design vibrotactile feedback by: (1) selecting actuators based on bandwidth and mounting requirements, (2) designing tactile patterns that are discriminable and learnable, (3) leveraging tactile illusions for efficient communication, (4) ensuring patterns are distinct enough to differentiate without confusion, (5) testing with users to validate pattern recognition.
