# Haptic Communication

## Overview
Haptic communication uses touch as a medium for transmitting information between humans and machines, or between humans through machines. C9-AETHER provides semantic framing while C16-VOXUM articulates the communication protocol.

## Core Concepts
- **Haptic Alphabet**: A set of distinct tactile signals with assigned meanings. Can be based on location (which actuator fires), rhythm (temporal pattern), intensity, or combinations. Like a tactile language.
- **Social Touch**: Touch conveys emotioncomfort, urgency, warning, approval. The same tactile signal can have different meanings depending on context, relationship, and intensity.
- **Attention Direction**: Haptic cues can direct attention to events, locations, or actions. A vibration on the left side of a wearable indicates a left-side event; a pulsing pattern indicates urgency level.

## Application
Design haptic communication by: (1) defining a haptic vocabulary with clear signal-meaning mappings, (2) considering emotional and social dimensions of touch signals, (3) using spatial cues for directional information, (4) ensuring patterns are discriminable under real-world conditions, (5) documenting the haptic language for user training.
