# Haptic Interface Design

## Overview
Haptic interface design is the practice of creating effective, intuitive, and safe touch-based interfaces for humans. C15-LUMINARIS brings clarity to the user experience while C22-AURELION ensures aesthetic quality.

## Core Concepts
- **User-Centered Design**: Haptic interfaces must be designed around human perceptual capabilities and limitations. Two-point discrimination threshold, force sensitivity, vibration frequency sensitivity, and adaptation rates all inform design decisions.
- **Multimodal Integration**: Haptics rarely works in isolationcoordinate with visual and auditory channels. Haptic cues can replace, supplement, or reinforce other modalities depending on the task and context.
- **Safety and Comfort**: Excessive force, prolonged vibration, or unexpected movements can cause discomfort or injury. Implement force limits, vibration exposure limits, and emergency stops as safety requirements.

## Application
Design haptic interfaces by: (1) understanding human haptic perception capabilities relevant to the application, (2) designing interactions that are intuitive and learnable, (3) coordinating haptic feedback with visual and auditory presentation, (4) implementing safety limits for force and vibration, (5) testing with representative users and iterating based on feedback.
