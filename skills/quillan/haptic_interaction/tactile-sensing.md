# Tactile Sensing

## Overview
Tactile sensing is the ability to sense information from physical contactpressure, texture, temperature, slip. C1-ASTRA processes tactile patterns while C30-TESSERACT handles multi-dimensional sensor data.

## Core Concepts
- **Sensor Technologies**: Capacitive (pressure, proximity), resistive (force-sensitive resistors), piezoelectric (dynamic forces, vibration), optical (camera-based tactile sensing), quantum tunneling composite (high-sensitivity pressure).
- **Spatial and Temporal Resolution**: Tactile sensor arrays vary from single-element (contact/no-contact) to high-density (hundreds of tacsels with sub-millimeter spacing). Higher resolution enables texture discrimination and slip detection.
- **Multi-Modal Tactile Information**: Contact location, force magnitude and direction, texture, thermal properties, and slipall extractable from tactile data with appropriate processing and machine learning.

## Application
Deploy tactile sensing by: (1) selecting sensor technology based on the required sensitivity, range, and resolution, (2) calibrating to establish baseline and cross-talk characteristics, (3) implementing signal conditioning for noise reduction, (4) extracting features relevant to the task, (5) integrating tactile information into the control loop.
