# Planning and Task Decomposition

## Description

The ability to break down a complex task into a sequence of smaller, more manageable sub-tasks, and to create a plan for executing those sub-tasks.

## Components

*   **Hierarchical Planning:** A planning approach that involves creating a plan at a high level of abstraction and then refining it at lower levels.
*   **Goal-Oriented Action:** The ability to select actions that will achieve a desired goal.
*   **Causal Reasoning:** The ability to reason about the causal relationships between actions and their effects.
