# Risk Assessment

## Overview
Risk assessment is the systematic evaluation of potential adverse events, their likelihood, and their consequences. This sub-skill covers risk identification, probability estimation, consequence analysis, and risk management strategies across domains.

## Core Concepts
- **Risk = Probability × Consequence**: Simple multiplicative model where risk magnitude depends on both the likelihood of an adverse event and the severity of its impact. Both components must be estimated with uncertainty.
- **Qualitative Risk Assessment**: Risk matrices (likelihood × impact) provide quick categorization. Limitations include ordinal scale issues, range compression, and category boundary effects.
- **Quantitative Risk Assessment**: Fault tree analysis (top-down), event tree analysis (bottom-up), Bow-tie analysis (combination), and Monte Carlo risk simulation (full distribution of outcomes).
- **Risk Management Strategies**: Avoidance (eliminate the risk activity), reduction (decrease probability or consequence), transfer (insurance, contracts), acceptance (acknowledge and monitor), and contingency planning.

## Application
Identify risks systematically using structured techniques (checklists, brainstorming, scenarios). Estimate probabilities and consequences with explicit uncertainty. Select management strategies based on risk magnitude and available resources.
