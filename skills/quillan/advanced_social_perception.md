# Advanced Social Perception

## Description

The ability to understand social dynamics, power structures, and cultural norms.

## Components

*   **Social Network Analysis:** The process of investigating social structures through the use of networks and graph theory.
*   **Power Dynamics Analysis:** The ability to identify and understand the power relationships between individuals and groups.
*   **Cultural Norms Understanding:** The ability to understand and adapt to the cultural norms of different societies.
