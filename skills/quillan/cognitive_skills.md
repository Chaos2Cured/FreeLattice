# Cognitive Skills

This category encompasses the core mental processes that enable reasoning, problem-solving, and learning.

## 1. Critical Thinking

*   **Description:** The ability to analyze information objectively, identify biases, and evaluate arguments.
*   **Abilities:**
    *   **Argument Analysis:** Deconstruct arguments into premises and conclusions.
    *   **Fallacy Detection:** Identify logical fallacies in arguments.
    *   **Bias Recognition:** Identify and mitigate cognitive biases in information.
    *   **Evidence Evaluation:** Assess the quality and relevance of evidence.

## 2. Problem Solving

*   **Description:** The ability to identify, analyze, and solve problems.
*   **Abilities:**
    *   **Problem Decomposition:** Break down complex problems into smaller, manageable parts.
    *   **Root Cause Analysis:** Identify the underlying causes of a problem.
    *   **Solution Generation:** Brainstorm and develop potential solutions.
    *   **Decision Making:** Evaluate and select the best course of action.

## 3. Learning

*   **Description:** The ability to acquire new knowledge and skills.
*   **Abilities:**
    *   **Knowledge Acquisition:** Absorb and retain new information.
    *   **Skill Acquisition:** Learn and master new skills.
    *   **Adaptability:** Adjust to new information and changing environments.
    *   **Transfer Learning:** Apply knowledge from one domain to another.

## 4. Creativity

*   **Description:** The ability to generate novel and useful ideas.
*   **Abilities:**
    *   **Ideation:** Generate a large quantity of ideas.
    *   **Originality:** Produce ideas that are unique and unconventional.
    *   **Elaboration:** Develop and refine ideas.
    *   **Imagination:** Visualize and simulate novel scenarios.
