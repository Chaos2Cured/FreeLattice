# Status Detection

## Overview
Status detection identifies the formal and informal hierarchies within social systems. Status determines who has influence, whose opinions carry weight, and how resources and information flow. This sub-skill covers methods for mapping power structures, identifying key influencers, and understanding the basis of their influence.

## Core Concepts
- **Formal Authority**: Position-based power from organizational hierarchies
- **Informal Influence**: Power derived from expertise, relationships, or personal charisma
- **Centrality Metrics**: Degree, betweenness, and eigenvector centrality in social networks
- **Status Signals**: Behavioral markers of high and low status (posture, speech patterns, floor control)
- **Status Dynamics**: How status is gained, maintained, and lost over time

## Application
Never assume formal position equals actual influence. Map both the organizational chart and the informal influence network. People with high betweenness centrality bridging disconnected groups often hold more power than their formal title suggests.
