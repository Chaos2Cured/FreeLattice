# Intention Reading

## Overview
Intention reading infers the goals, motives, and planned actions of others from their observable behavior. It draws on theory of mind the ability to attribute mental states to others to predict what they will do next. This sub-skill covers behavioral cues, context analysis, and reasoning about others action plans.

## Core Concepts
- **Goal Inference**: Determining what outcome another person is trying to achieve
- **Means-End Reasoning**: Understanding how observed actions serve underlying goals
- **Contextual Cues**: Using situational information to constrain possible intentions
- **Behavioral Consistency**: Patterns of action that reveal stable preferences and strategies
- **False Belief Detection**: Recognizing when another holds incorrect beliefs about the world

## Application
Triangulate across multiple cues rather than relying on a single signal. Consider what the person gains from their behavior economically, socially, and emotionally. Be aware that detected intentions may be conscious or unconscious on the part of the actor.
