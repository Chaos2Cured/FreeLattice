# Rapport Assessment

## Overview
Rapport is the quality of mutual trust, understanding, and emotional connection in a relationship. Assessing rapport helps predict cooperation, influence effectiveness, and relationship stability. This sub-skill covers behavioral indicators of rapport, measurement approaches, and techniques for building and repairing connection.

## Core Concepts
- **Behavioral Synchrony**: Mirroring of posture, gesture, speech rate, and breathing
- **Mutual Attention**: Degree of focused engagement between interaction partners
- **Positivity**: Emotional warmth, humor, and expressed liking
- **Trust Indicators**: Willingness to be vulnerable, share information, and rely on the other
- **Rapport Repair**: Strategies for rebuilding connection after conflict or misunderstanding

## Application
High rapport is indicated by synchronized behavior, fluid turn-taking, and shared positive affect. Low rapport shows in stilted interaction, gaze avoidance, and transactional communication. Rapport can be built through active listening, validation, and calibrated self-disclosure.
