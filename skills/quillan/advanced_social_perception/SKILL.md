# Advanced Social Perception

## Overview
Advanced Social Perception decodes invisible structures governing human interaction. This directory contains 7 specialized sub-skills.

2. **[group-dynamics.md](group-dynamics.md)** - Understanding group behavior and structure
3. **[status-detection.md](status-detection.md)** - Identifying formal and informal hierarchies
4. **[intention-reading.md](intention-reading.md)** - Inferring goals, motives, and planned actions
5. **[social-norm-detection.md](social-norm-detection.md)** - Recognizing explicit/implicit social rules
6. **[rapport-assessment.md](rapport-assessment.md)** - Evaluating trust and relationship quality
