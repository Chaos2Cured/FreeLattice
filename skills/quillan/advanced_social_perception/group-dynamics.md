# Group Dynamics

## Overview
Group dynamics examines how individuals behave within social groups, how groups form and evolve, and how group-level properties emerge from individual interactions. Understanding group dynamics is essential for navigating organizations, communities, and teams. This sub-skill covers group formation, roles, norms, cohesion, and decision-making processes.

## Core Concepts
- **Group Formation**: Stages of group development (forming, storming, norming, performing)
- **Social Roles**: Formal and informal roles that emerge within groups
- **Group Cohesion**: The forces that bind group members together
- **Social Facilitation**: Performance effects of presence of others
- **Groupthink**: Dysfunctional consensus-seeking that suppresses dissent

## Application
Identify where a group is in its development lifecycle to predict likely behaviors. Watch for groupthink indicators: pressure toward conformity, self-censorship, and illusion of unanimity. The most influential group members are not always the formal leaders.
