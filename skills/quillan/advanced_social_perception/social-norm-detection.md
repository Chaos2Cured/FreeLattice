# Social Norm Detection

## Overview
Social norms are the unwritten rules that govern behavior within groups and cultures. Detecting these norms is essential for navigating unfamiliar social environments without causing offense or misunderstanding. This sub-skill covers methods for identifying explicit and implicit rules, taboos, and expected behaviors.

## Core Concepts
- **Explicit Norms**: Formally stated rules, laws, and codes of conduct
- **Implicit Norms**: Unwritten expectations learned through observation and socialization
- **Taboo Detection**: Identifying topics and behaviors that violate cultural boundaries
- **Norm Violation Signals**: Reactions (surprise, disapproval, embarrassment) that reveal hidden norms
- **Code-Switching**: Adapting behavior across different normative contexts

## Application
When entering an unfamiliar social context, observe what behaviors trigger negative reactions. The strength of reaction reveals the importance of the norm. Violations of sacred values produce stronger reactions than violations of conventional preferences.
