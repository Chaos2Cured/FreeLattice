# Analogical Reasoning

Powerful cognitive process that identifies structural similarities between disparate domains, enabling knowledge transfer from familiar contexts to unfamiliar ones. Provides structured protocols for the complete analogical reasoning cycle — from source domain selection through mapping, inference, and validation.

## Sub-Skills
1. Source-Target Mapping — Identifying systematic correspondences between domains
2. Cross-Domain Transfer — Applying knowledge across domain boundaries
3. Metaphor Generation — Creating novel conceptual mappings for expression and insight
4. Structural Alignment — Finding relational correspondences between domain structures
5. Analogy Evaluation — Assessing validity, utility, and boundaries of analogies
6. Creative Analogies — Generating novel connections for innovation and discovery
7. Case-Based Reasoning — Solving new problems by adapting solutions from past cases
