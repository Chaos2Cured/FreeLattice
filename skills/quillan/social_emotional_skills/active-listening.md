# Active Listening

## Overview
Active listening is a structured communication technique that involves fully concentrating on, understanding, responding to, and remembering what is being said. It goes beyond passive hearing to include verbal and non-verbal feedback, paraphrasing, reflecting feelings, asking clarifying questions, summarizing, and withholding judgment. Active listening builds trust and deepens understanding.

## Core Concepts
- **Attending Behaviors**: Eye contact, body orientation, nodding, and verbal acknowledgments showing engagement
- **Paraphrasing and Reflecting**: Restating content and reflecting perceived emotions to confirm understanding
- **Clarifying Questions**: Asking open-ended questions to deepen understanding without leading
- **Summarizing and Synthesizing**: Consolidating key points to confirm shared understanding and move forward

## Application
Apply active listening for customer service, therapeutic AI, educational tutoring, conflict mediation, interview systems, and any interaction requiring deep understanding of user needs.
