# Conflict Resolution

## Overview
Conflict resolution is the process of addressing disagreements constructively to reach mutually acceptable outcomes. It covers conflict styles (competing, accommodating, avoiding, collaborating, compromising), negotiation principles (separating people from problems, focusing on interests not positions), de-escalation techniques, mediation, and post-conflict relationship repair.

## Core Concepts
- **Conflict Styles**: Different approaches to conflict (competing, collaborating, compromising, avoiding, accommodating)
- **Principled Negotiation**: Focusing on interests rather than positions; generating options for mutual gain
- **De-Escalation**: Techniques for reducing emotional intensity and preventing conflict escalation
- **Mediation and Third-Party Intervention**: Facilitating resolution between conflicting parties

## Application
Use conflict resolution for designing AI mediators, customer complaint handling, team collaboration tools, negotiation support systems, and any system managing inter-human conflict.
