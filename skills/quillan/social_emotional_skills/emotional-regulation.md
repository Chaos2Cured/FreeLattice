# Emotional Regulation

## Overview
Emotional regulation refers to the processes by which individuals manage which emotions they have, when they have them, and how they experience and express them. Strategies include situation selection, situation modification, attentional deployment, cognitive change (reappraisal), and response modulation. Effective regulation is essential for mental health, social functioning, and decision-making.

## Core Concepts
- **Cognitive Reappraisal**: Reframing the meaning of a situation to alter its emotional impact
- **Expressive Suppression**: Inhibiting the outward expression of emotion (less effective long-term)
- **Attentional Deployment**: Directing attention toward or away from emotion-eliciting aspects
- **Situation Selection and Modification**: Choosing or changing contexts to manage emotional exposure

## Application
Use emotional regulation strategies for designing AI that responds appropriately under pressure, managing virtual agent behavior, therapeutic applications, and creating systems that handle emotionally charged interactions.
