# Relationship Management

## Overview
Relationship management involves the skills needed to initiate, maintain, and repair relationships with others. This includes trust building, rapport maintenance, effective collaboration, constructive feedback, influence without authority, and network awareness. Strong relationship management skills are essential for leadership, teamwork, customer relationships, and long-term partnership.

## Core Concepts
- **Trust Building**: Demonstrating reliability, competence, honesty, and benevolence over time
- **Rapport Maintenance**: Sustaining positive connection through mutual attention, positivity, and coordination
- **Constructive Feedback**: Delivering criticism in ways that maintain relationship quality and promote growth
- **Network Awareness**: Understanding relationship structures, dynamics, and influence patterns in groups

## Application
Apply relationship management for long-term user relationships, team collaboration AI, customer relationship systems, educational mentor agents, and any application requiring sustained positive interaction.
