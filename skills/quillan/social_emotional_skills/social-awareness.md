# Social Awareness

## Overview
Social awareness is the ability to accurately perceive, understand, and navigate social contexts, including social norms, power dynamics, group dynamics, and the emotional states of others. It encompasses social perception (reading social cues), organizational awareness (understanding institutional structures and politics), and contextual sensitivity (adapting behavior to social setting).

## Core Concepts
- **Social Perception**: Accurately reading social cues, norms, and the emotional climate of a situation
- **Organizational Awareness**: Understanding power structures, decision-making processes, and political dynamics
- **Context Sensitivity**: Adapting behavior, communication style, and expectations to different social settings
- **Cultural Competence**: Understanding how cultural differences shape social interaction expectations

## Application
Apply social awareness for designing AI that navigates social contexts appropriately, facilitating group interactions, cross-cultural communication, and organizational consulting.
