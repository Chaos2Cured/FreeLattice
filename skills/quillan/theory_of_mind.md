# Theory of Mind

## Description

The ability to attribute mental states—beliefs, intents, desires, emotions, knowledge, etc.—to oneself and to others, and to understand that others have beliefs, desires, intentions, and perspectives that are different from one's own.

## Components

*   **Belief Attribution:** The ability to infer the beliefs of others.
*   **Intention Recognition:** The ability to infer the intentions of others.
*   **Emotion Recognition:** The ability to infer the emotions of others.
