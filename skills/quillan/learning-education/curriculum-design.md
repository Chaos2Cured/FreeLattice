# Curriculum Design
## Overview
Curriculum design is the systematic process of creating educational programs, courses, and learning experiences that achieve specific learning outcomes. This sub-skill covers needs analysis, learning objective formulation, content sequencing, and assessment alignment.
## Core Concepts
- **Backward Design**: Starting with desired outcomes and working backward to determine acceptable evidence and learning activities
- **Scope and Sequence**: Determining the breadth and ordering of content across a course or program
- **Prerequisite Mapping**: Identifying knowledge and skills that must precede new learning
- **Alignment**: Ensuring objectives, activities, and assessments are coherently connected
## Application
Apply curriculum design principles when developing courses, training programs, workshops, or any structured educational experience.
