# Student Engagement
## Overview
Student engagement refers to the degree of attention, curiosity, interest, and passion that learners show during instruction. This sub-skill covers behavioral, cognitive, and emotional engagement dimensions, and strategies for fostering active participation.
## Core Concepts
- **Behavioral Engagement**: Observable participation in academic and extracurricular activities
- **Cognitive Engagement**: Deep mental investment in learning, including use of learning strategies
- **Emotional Engagement**: Affective reactions to learning including interest, enjoyment, and belonging
- **Self-Determination Theory**: Autonomy, competence, and relatedness as foundations for intrinsic motivation
## Application
Use engagement strategies when designing lessons, developing educational technology, or addressing learner disengagement in any educational context.
