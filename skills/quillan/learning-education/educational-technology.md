# Educational Technology
## Overview
Educational technology encompasses the use of digital tools, platforms, and systems to enhance teaching and learning. This sub-skill covers learning management systems, adaptive learning platforms, educational software evaluation, and technology integration frameworks.
## Core Concepts
- **SAMR Model**: Substitution, Augmentation, Modification, Redefinition as levels of technology integration
- **Learning Management Systems**: Platforms for organizing, delivering, and tracking educational content
- **Adaptive Learning**: Technology that adjusts instruction based on individual learner performance
- **Multimedia Learning**: Using text, images, audio, and video to optimize cognitive processing
## Application
Use this when selecting educational technology, designing online learning experiences, or integrating digital tools into instructional practice.
