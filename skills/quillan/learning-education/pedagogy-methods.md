# Pedagogy Methods
## Overview
Pedagogy is the theory and practice of teaching. This sub-skill covers major instructional approaches including direct instruction, inquiry-based learning, project-based learning, flipped classroom, and differentiated instruction strategies.
## Core Concepts
- **Direct Instruction**: Explicit teaching of concepts through structured presentation and guided practice
- **Inquiry-Based Learning**: Learning driven by student questions and investigations
- **Project-Based Learning**: Extended learning through authentic, meaningful projects
- **Flipped Classroom**: Students engage with instructional content at home and apply knowledge in class
## Application
Use this when designing instructional approaches for different contexts, selecting teaching methods aligned with learning goals, or evaluating the effectiveness of pedagogical strategies.
