# Differentiated Instruction
## Overview
Differentiated instruction is a teaching approach that proactively adapts instruction to meet the diverse learning needs of students. This sub-skill covers differentiation of content, process, product, and learning environment to accommodate varied readiness levels, interests, and learning profiles.
## Core Concepts
- **Content Differentiation**: Varying what students learn based on readiness and interest
- **Process Differentiation**: Varying how students make sense of ideas and information
- **Product Differentiation**: Varying how students demonstrate what they have learned
- **Tiered Assignments**: Creating different versions of an assignment at varying difficulty levels
## Application
Apply differentiated instruction when teaching diverse groups of learners, designing inclusive classrooms, or creating personalized learning experiences.
