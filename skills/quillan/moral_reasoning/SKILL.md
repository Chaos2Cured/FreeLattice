# Moral Reasoning

A thinking process for determining whether an action or policy is right or wrong. Covers the descriptive psychology of moral cognition — how people actually make moral judgments — along with normative ethical frameworks, moral foundations, and applied ethics protocols for rigorous ethical deliberation.

## Sub-Skills
1. Ethical Frameworks — Major normative theories and multi-perspective analysis
2. Justice and Fairness — Distributive, procedural, retributive, and restorative justice
3. Virtue Ethics — Character-based ethics and flourishing
4. Consequentialist Thinking — Outcome-based ethical evaluation
5. Deontological Reasoning — Duty and rule-based ethical frameworks
6. Moral Intuition — Fast, automatic moral responses and their calibration
7. Applied Ethics — Domain-specific ethics for technology, medicine, business
