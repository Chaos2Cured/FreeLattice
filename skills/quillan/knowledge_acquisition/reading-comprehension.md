# Reading Comprehension
## Overview
Reading comprehension is the ability to process text, understand its meaning, and integrate it with existing knowledge. This sub-skill covers literal comprehension, inferential understanding, critical reading, and strategies for deep text processing.
## Core Concepts
- **Literal Comprehension**: Understanding what the text explicitly states
- **Inferential Understanding**: Reading between the lines to grasp implied meaning
- **Critical Reading**: Evaluating claims, evidence, and reasoning in the text
- **Structural Analysis**: Understanding how the text is organized and how sections relate
## Application
Use reading comprehension strategies when analyzing complex documents, conducting literature reviews, extracting insights from technical texts, or building NLP systems for text understanding.
