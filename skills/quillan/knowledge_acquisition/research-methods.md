# Research Methods
## Overview
Research methods provide systematic approaches to investigating questions and generating new knowledge. This sub-skill covers qualitative and quantitative research designs, experimental methodology, literature review strategies, and research ethics.
## Core Concepts
- **Research Design**: Choosing between experimental, correlational, descriptive, and qualitative approaches
- **Hypothesis Testing**: Formulating falsifiable hypotheses and testing them with appropriate statistical methods
- **Systematic Review**: Methodically aggregating and synthesizing findings from multiple studies
- **Research Ethics**: Informed consent, privacy protection, and responsible conduct of research
## Application
Apply research methods when conducting original investigations, evaluating existing research, designing experiments, or writing academic papers.
