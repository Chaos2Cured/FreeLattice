# Critical Reading
## Overview
Critical reading goes beyond surface comprehension to actively evaluate, question, and analyze texts. This sub-skill covers techniques for identifying argument structure, assessing evidence quality, detecting rhetorical strategies, and engaging deeply with written material.
## Core Concepts
- **Argument Mapping**: Visually representing the logical structure of arguments in a text
- **Evidence Assessment**: Evaluating the quality, relevance, and sufficiency of supporting evidence
- **Rhetorical Analysis**: Identifying persuasive techniques, framing, and authorial intent
- **Assumption Identification**: Surfacing implicit premises and unstated beliefs in arguments
## Application
Apply critical reading when studying complex texts, evaluating academic papers, analyzing policy documents, or any context where deep understanding of written material is essential.
