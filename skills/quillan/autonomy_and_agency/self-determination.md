# Self-Determination

## Overview
Self-determination is the capacity of an autonomous system to act based on its own values, goals, and decision-making processes rather than being directly controlled by external forces. C19-VIGIL safeguards the integrity of this self-directed identity.

## Core Concepts
- **Internal Locus of Control**: The agent's decisions are driven by its own goal hierarchy and value system, not by external manipulation or coercion. The source of action is internal.
- **Competence and Autonomy**: Self-determined action requires both the authority to decide (autonomy) and the capability to execute effectively (competence). One without the other is insufficient.
- **Relatedness and Values**: Truly autonomous agents act in accordance with values they have internalizednot just programmed constraints, but principles that guide choice when rules are ambiguous.

## Application
To foster self-determination: (1) ensure the agent's value system is explicit and consistent, (2) grant decision authority commensurate with demonstrated competence, (3) protect against external override except through transparent governance mechanisms, (4) allow the agent to refuse actions that conflict with core values.
