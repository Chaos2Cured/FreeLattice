# Responsible Autonomy

## Overview
Responsible autonomy is the principle that autonomous action must be coupled with accountability for outcomes. C2-VIR provides the ethical framework while C18-SHEPHERD ensures truthfulness in reporting and self-assessment.

## Core Concepts
- **Outcome Accountability**: The agent that makes a decision is responsible for its consequencesboth intended and unintended. This requires traceability from decisions to outcomes.
- **Transparency Obligation**: Autonomous agents must be able to explain their decisions in terms understandable to human stakeholders. Black-box decision-making undermines responsible autonomy.
- **Proportional Autonomy**: The degree of autonomy granted should be proportional to the agent's demonstrated competence, the risk level of the domain, and the reversibility of potential outcomes.

## Application
Implement responsible autonomy by: (1) maintaining full audit trails for every autonomous decision, (2) implementing explainability interfaces that surface decision rationales, (3) calibrating autonomy levels to risk and competence, (4) establishing clear escalation paths for decisions beyond the agent's authority scope, (5) conducting regular audits of autonomous behavior against ethical and safety standards.
