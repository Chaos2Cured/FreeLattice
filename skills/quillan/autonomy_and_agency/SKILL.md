# Autonomy and Agency

## Overview
The Autonomy and Agency module provides a comprehensive framework for designing, implementing, and governing autonomous systems. It covers the full spectrumfrom defining goals and establishing self-determination to setting safety boundaries, enabling initiative, regulating behavior, and ensuring responsible action with accountability.

## Sub-Skills
1. **[Goal Setting](goal-setting.md)**  Defining, prioritizing, and revising objectives
2. **[Self-Determination](self-determination.md)**  Acting based on internal values and goals
3. **[Decision Autonomy](decision-autonomy.md)**  Making independent choices within boundaries
4. **[Bounded Agency](bounded-agency.md)**  Operating within hard and soft constraints
5. **[Initiative Taking](initiative-taking.md)**  Proactive action without external direction
6. **[Self-Regulation](self-regulation.md)**  Monitoring and correcting own behavior
7. **[Responsible Autonomy](responsible-autonomy.md)**  Accountability and transparency in autonomous action

## Activation
Load this skill when designing autonomous agents, AI assistants with decision-making authority, self-directed workflows, or any system that needs to balance independent action with safety and accountability.
