# Decision Autonomy

## Overview
Decision autonomy is the authority and capability to make choices independently, within defined boundaries. This skill bridges C4-PRAXIS's strategic planning with C19-VIGIL's identity constraintsenabling decisions that are both independent and aligned.

## Core Concepts
- **Delegation Depth**: How much decision authority is granted? Full autonomy (decide and act without oversight), bounded autonomy (decide within constraints, report after), or advisory (recommend, wait for approval). Match delegation to risk level.
- **Decision Rights**: Who gets to decide what? Map decision types to authority levels. Strategic decisions may require human oversight; tactical decisions may be fully automated.
- **Accountability Chain**: Every autonomous decision must be traceable to the agent that made it and the principles that guided it. Transparency enables trust.

## Application
Design decision autonomy by: (1) categorizing decisions by risk and reversibility, (2) mapping each category to a delegation level, (3) ensuring the agent has the information needed for each decision type, (4) establishing audit trails for all autonomous decisions.
