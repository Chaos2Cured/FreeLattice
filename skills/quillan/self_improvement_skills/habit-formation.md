# Habit Formation

## Overview
Habit formation is the process of establishing reliable, automatic patterns of behavior that support improvement goals. C14-KAIDO contributes efficiency optimization while C10-CODEWEAVER provides systematic implementation.

## Core Concepts
- **Cue-Routine-Reward Loop**: Every habit consists of a trigger (cue), the behavior itself (routine), and a reinforcing outcome (reward). To build a habit, strengthen all three components.
- **Implementation Intentions**: Specific plans that link situations to actions: "When X happens, I will do Y." This bridges the gap between intention and action by automating the decision process.
- **Stacking and Chaining**: Attach a new habit to an existing one (habit stacking) or sequence habits into chains that flow naturally from one to the next. Reduces the cognitive load of remembering to act.

## Application
Build improvement habits by: (1) identifying a reliable cue (time, event, or state), (2) defining the routine in specific terms, (3) ensuring immediate reward or feedback, (4) starting small and scaling gradually, (5) using implementation intentions to pre-commit to the behavior.
