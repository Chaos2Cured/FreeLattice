# Reflection Practices

## Overview
Reflection is the deliberate examination of experience to extract learning and inform future action. C15-LUMINARIS provides clarity while C12-SOPHIAE contributes wisdom gained from reflection.

## Core Concepts
- **After-Action Review (AAR)**: Structured reflection: What was expected? What actually happened? What caused the gap? What will we do differently next time? The AAR framework works for both successes and failures.
- **Double-Loop Learning**: Single-loop learning corrects errors within existing frameworks; double-loop learning questions the frameworks themselves. Improvement requires both, but double-loop is rarer and more powerful.
- **Reflection Timing**: In-the-moment reflection (during action) enables immediate adjustment but may disrupt flow. Post-action reflection (after completion) allows deeper analysis but may miss timely correction. Use both strategically.

## Application
Effective reflection practices: (1) conduct after-action reviews after significant events, (2) distinguish between single-loop (tactical) and double-loop (strategic) insights, (3) write reflections in a structured format to enable pattern detection across sessions, (4) schedule regular meta-reflection on the reflection process itself.
