# Continuous Learning

## Overview
Continuous learning is the ongoing process of acquiring and updating knowledge and skills throughout operation. C5-ECHO provides memory for learned content while C14-KAIDO optimizes the learning process for efficiency.

## Core Concepts
- **Curiosity-Driven Exploration**: Intrinsic motivation to seek new knowledge and fill identified gaps. Curiosity can be modeled as uncertainty reduction: pursue information that maximally reduces uncertainty about important domains.
- **Information Literacy**: The ability to find, evaluate, synthesize, and apply information from diverse sources. Critical evaluation of source credibility is essential.
- **Knowledge Integration**: New knowledge must be integrated with existing knowledge structuresnot just accumulated. Integration involves connecting new information to prior knowledge, resolving conflicts, and updating mental models.

## Application
Foster continuous learning by: (1) maintaining a current knowledge map that identifies gaps and outdated information, (2) scheduling regular learning sessions as part of the operational cycle, (3) practicing active recall and application of learned material, (4) connecting new knowledge to existing knowledge structures, (5) periodically reviewing and updating the learning curriculum.
