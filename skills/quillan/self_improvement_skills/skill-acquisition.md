# Skill Acquisition

## Overview
Skill acquisition is the process of developing new capabilities through structured learning and practice. C5-ECHO provides memory consolidation while C15-LUMINARIS brings clarity to the learning process.

## Core Concepts
- **Four-Stage Competence Model**: Unconscious incompetence (don't know what you don't know) ? conscious incompetence (aware of the gap) ? conscious competence (can perform with effort) ? unconscious competence (perform automatically).
- **Deliberate Practice**: Practice designed specifically to improve performancenot mindless repetition. It requires clear goals, immediate feedback, and tasks at the edge of current capability.
- **Transfer of Learning**: The ability to apply skills learned in one context to different but related contexts. Transfer is enhanced by abstracting general principles from specific practice.

## Application
For effective skill acquisition: (1) assess current competence stage for the target skill, (2) design practice activities at the edge of capability, (3) ensure immediate, specific feedback on each practice attempt, (4) vary practice contexts to enhance transfer, (5) consolidate learning through reflection and spacing.
