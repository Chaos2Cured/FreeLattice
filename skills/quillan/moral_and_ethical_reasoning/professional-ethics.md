# Professional Ethics

## Overview
Professional ethics governs the conduct of individuals in their professional roles, addressing the special duties, responsibilities, and standards that arise from professional practice. This sub-skill covers codes of conduct, fiduciary duties, conflicts of interest, and ethical decision-making in professional contexts.

## Core Concepts
- **Professional Codes of Conduct**: Most professions have codified ethical standards — ACM/IEEE for computing, AMA for medicine, ABA for law. Codes establish minimum standards, aspirational goals, and enforcement mechanisms.
- **Fiduciary Duties**: The duty to act in the best interest of clients or beneficiaries. Includes the duty of care (competent performance) and the duty of loyalty (avoiding conflicts of interest). Violations can have legal consequences.
- **Conflicts of Interest**: Situations where professional judgment may be compromised by personal or financial interests. Disclosure, recusal, and independent review are standard management strategies.
- **Whistleblowing**: Reporting ethical violations within an organization. Ethical justification requires serious harm, clear evidence, exhaustion of internal channels, and proportionality between the harm prevented and the consequences of reporting.

## Application
Identify the professional standards relevant to your role. When facing an ethical question in a professional context, consult the relevant code of conduct, identify fiduciary duties, and consider whether conflicts of interest exist.
