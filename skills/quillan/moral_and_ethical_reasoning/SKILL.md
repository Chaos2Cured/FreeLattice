# Moral and Ethical Reasoning

Provides the frameworks and protocols for systematically analyzing questions of right and wrong, good and bad, duty and consequence. Covers major normative ethical traditions, applied ethics domains, and practical protocols for structured ethical deliberation in organizational, technological, and professional contexts.

## Sub-Skills
1. Ethical Dilemma Resolution — Structured navigation of conflicting moral principles
2. Stakeholder Analysis — Identifying and weighing interests of all affected parties
3. Moral Judgment — Psychology of moral evaluation and cognitive biases
4. Ethical Decision Frameworks — Step-by-step protocols for ethical reasoning
5. Value Alignment — Ensuring AI systems and organizations act on human values
6. Professional Ethics — Standards and duties within professional roles
7. Cross-Cultural Ethics — Ethical reasoning across cultural boundaries
