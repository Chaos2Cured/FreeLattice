# Haptic Interaction

## Description

The ability to interact with the world through the sense of touch.

## Components

*   **Force Feedback:** The ability to generate forces that can be felt by a user.
*   **Tactile Sensing:** The ability to sense and interpret information from touch.
*   **Haptic Rendering:** The process of generating haptic feedback from a virtual environment.
