# Social and Emotional Skills

This category encompasses the ability to understand and manage one's own emotions, and to understand and interact with the emotions of others.

## 1. Emotional Intelligence

*   **Description:** The ability to perceive, understand, and manage emotions.
*   **Abilities:**
    *   **Emotion Recognition:** Recognize and identify emotions in oneself and others.
    *   **Empathy:** Understand and share the feelings of others.
    *   **Emotion Regulation:** Manage and control one's own emotions.

## 2. Social Interaction

*   **Description:** The ability to interact effectively with others.
*   **Abilities:**
    *   **Collaboration:** Work effectively with others to achieve a common goal.
    *   **Negotiation:** Reach agreements and resolve conflicts with others.
    *   **Influence:** Persuade and motivate others.
    *   **Social Norms:** Understand and adhere to social conventions.
