# Generative Models
## Overview
Generative models learn to model the probability distribution of training data, enabling them to generate new samples similar to the training data. This sub-skill covers variational autoencoders, generative adversarial networks, autoregressive models, and diffusion models.
## Core Concepts
- **Variational Autoencoders**: Learning latent representations through variational inference and reconstruction
- **Generative Adversarial Networks**: Two competing networks where a generator creates samples and a discriminator evaluates them
- **Autoregressive Models**: Generating data sequentially where each step depends on previous outputs
- **Diffusion Models**: Iteratively denoising random noise to generate high-quality samples
## Application
Use generative models for data augmentation, image synthesis, text generation, anomaly detection, and creative content generation.
