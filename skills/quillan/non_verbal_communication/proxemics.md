# Proxemics

## Overview
Proxemics is the study of how people use and perceive space during communication and social interaction. Edward T. Hall identified four distance zones: intimate (0-18 inches), personal (18 inches-4 feet), social (4-12 feet), and public (12+ feet). Spatial behavior signals relationship type, cultural background, personality, and situational comfort level.

## Core Concepts
- **Distance Zones**: Intimate, personal, social, public  each with characteristic activities and sensory involvement
- **Territoriality**: Marking and defending personal space; primary, secondary, and public territories
- **Spatial Arrangements**: Seating choices, standing positions, and their social meanings
- **Cultural Variation**: Contact vs. non-contact cultures have different proxemic norms

## Application
Use proxemics for social robot positioning, virtual environment design, cross-cultural communication training, spatial user interfaces, and understanding social dynamics in physical and virtual spaces.
