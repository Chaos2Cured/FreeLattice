# Gestures

## Overview
Gestures are deliberate body movements, primarily of the hands and arms, that communicate meaning. They include emblems (culturally coded signals like thumbs-up), illustrators (speech-accompanying gestures that depict or emphasize), regulators (turn-taking signals), adaptors (self-touch behaviors), and deictic gestures (pointing). Gesture production and comprehension are closely linked to speech and thought.

## Core Concepts
- **Emblematic Gestures**: Culturally conventionalized gestures with direct verbal translations (OK, peace sign)
- **Illustrators**: Beat gestures (rhythmic), iconic (depicting shape/action), metaphoric (abstract concepts)
- **Adaptors**: Self-touch, object manipulation, and other tension-relieving or habitual movements
- **Gesture-Speech Integration**: Gestures and speech form a unified communication system; gestures often precede and predict speech

## Application
Use gesture analysis for embodied agent animation, human-robot interaction, communication assessment, cross-cultural training, and understanding multimodal communication.
