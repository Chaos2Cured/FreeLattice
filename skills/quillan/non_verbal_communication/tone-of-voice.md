# Tone of Voice

## Overview
Tone of voice, or vocal prosody, communicates emotional state, attitude, and pragmatic intent through pitch, rhythm, volume, pace, and voice quality. Paralanguage (non-verbal vocal elements) modifies meaning independent of lexical content. Prosody signals questions vs. statements, sarcasm, enthusiasm, uncertainty, authority, and emotional arousal.

## Core Concepts
- **Pitch and Intonation**: Fundamental frequency patterns conveying questions, statements, emotion, attitude
- **Rhythm and Pace**: Speech tempo, pausing patterns, syllable timing indicating cognitive state and emotion
- **Volume and Emphasis**: Loudness variation and stress patterns highlighting important content
- **Voice Quality**: Breathiness, creak, nasality, whisper conveying intimacy, authority, hesitation, or emotion

## Application
Apply tone of voice analysis for speech emotion recognition, expressive speech synthesis, intelligent virtual assistants, call center analytics, and any application processing spoken language.
