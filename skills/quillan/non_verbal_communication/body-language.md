# Body Language

## Overview
Body language encompasses the nonverbal signals conveyed through posture, stance, movement, and overall physical presentation. It includes open vs. closed postures, mirroring, tension indicators, orientation (toward or away from interaction partners), and congruence between different body regions. Body language communicates confidence, interest, nervousness, openness, and social status.

## Core Concepts
- **Posture Types**: Open (receptive, confident) vs. closed (defensive, uncomfortable) postures
- **Mirroring**: Unconscious imitation of another's posture, indicating rapport and alignment
- **Orientation and Lean**: Direction of body orientation and forward/backward lean signal engagement
- **Congruence**: Consistency between different body language channels and between verbal and nonverbal signals

## Application
Analyze body language for social interaction understanding, character animation, virtual agent behavior design, interview analysis, and human-robot interaction.
