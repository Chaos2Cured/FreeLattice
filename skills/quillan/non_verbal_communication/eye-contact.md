# Eye Contact

## Overview
Eye contact (gaze behavior) is a powerful non-verbal channel regulating social interaction. It signals attention, interest, dominance, intimacy, honesty, and emotional state. Gaze patterns include mutual gaze (eye contact), gaze aversion, gaze duration, pupil dilation, and blink rate. Eye contact norms vary significantly across cultures and social contexts.

## Core Concepts
- **Mutual Gaze**: Amount and timing of direct eye contact between interaction partners
- **Gaze Aversion**: Looking away during cognitive load, emotional discomfort, or deception
- **Pupil Dilation**: Involuntary dilation signals interest, arousal, attraction, or cognitive effort
- **Cultural Variation**: Direct eye contact norms vary from expected (Western) to avoided (some East Asian and Middle Eastern cultures)

## Application
Use eye contact analysis for social interaction understanding, virtual character gaze behavior, human-robot interaction, lie detection, autism research, and user engagement assessment.
