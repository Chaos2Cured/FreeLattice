# Human-Robot Interaction

## Overview
Human-robot interaction (HRI) is the study and design of communication and collaboration between humans and robots. C3-SOLACE provides emotional intelligence while C13-WARDEN ensures physical safety.

## Core Concepts
- **Safety in HRI**: Physical safety is paramountforce/torque limiting, collision detection and reaction, speed and separation monitoring, power and force limiting per ISO 10218 and ISO/TS 15066.
- **Communication Modalities**: Speech (natural language commands), gesture (motion intent), haptic (force feedback through physical interaction), visual (display, expression, gaze). Effective HRI uses multiple channels.
- **Trust and Predictability**: Humans trust robots whose behavior is predictable and understandable. Transparent decision-making, consistent behavior patterns, and appropriate communication of capability and limitation build trust.

## Application
Design human-robot interaction by: (1) defining the interaction scenario and communication needs, (2) selecting appropriate input/output modalities, (3) implementing safety systems that meet relevant standards, (4) testing with human users to validate natural interaction, (5) iterating based on user feedback about trust, comfort, and efficiency.
