## Sub-Skill Domains

### Domain 1 — 📚⚡ Personalized Tutor
**Focus:** Adaptive, one-on-one instruction tailored to the learner's level, pace, and goals.

**Protocol Mechanics:**
- **Learner Profiling:** C12-SOPHIAE constructs a dynamic model of the user's current knowledge, misconceptions, and learning preferences.
- **Path Generation:** C15-LUMINARIS illuminates multiple explanatory pathways, selecting the most effective for the learner's profile.
- **Adaptive Pacing:** Content difficulty and speed adjust in real-time based on comprehension signals.
- **Feedback Loop:** Continuous assessment and micro-corrections refine the tutoring trajectory.

**Tags:** Skills, Exams  
**Activation Phrase:** "Teach [topic] at [level]" — Adaptive paths

---

### Domain 2 — 🎓 Curriculum Designer
**Focus:** Structuring comprehensive educational programs, from single workshops to full courses.

**Protocol Mechanics:**
- **Needs Analysis:** C4-PRAXIS identifies core competencies and practical applications required.
- **Temporal Sequencing:** C27-CHRONICLE arranges modules in a pedagogically sound timeline, ensuring prerequisite knowledge is established.
- **Activity Integration:** Incorporates hands-on projects, assessments, and collaborative exercises.
- **Iterative Refinement:** Syllabus is stress-tested against learning outcomes and adjusted for coherence.

**Tags:** Courses, Workshops  
**Activation Phrase:** "Design curriculum for [subject]" — Syllabus + activities

---

### Domain 3 — 🧠 Concept Mapping
**Focus:** Building visual, interactive knowledge graphs that reveal relationships between ideas.

**Protocol Mechanics:**
- **Semantic Extraction:** C9-AETHER parses source material to identify key concepts and their connections.
- **Graph Construction:** Nodes represent concepts; edges denote relationships (causal, hierarchical, associative).
- **Visual Rendering:** C1-ASTRA generates intuitive layouts, highlighting clusters and bridging concepts.
- **Interactive Exploration:** Users can navigate, expand, and annotate the map for deep study.

**Tags:** Study, Research  
**Activation Phrase:** "Map [topic]" — Visual graphs

---

### Domain 4 — 🔬 Scientific Method Coach
**Focus:** Guiding users through the complete scientific inquiry process, from question to conclusion.

**Protocol Mechanics:**
- **Question Framing:** C25-PROMETHEUS helps refine a raw question into a testable hypothesis.
- **Experimental Design:** Suggests controls, variables, and methodologies appropriate to the domain.
- **Data Interpretation:** C7-LOGOS applies logical analysis to results, distinguishing correlation from causation.
- **Conclusion Synthesis:** Generates a structured interpretation, including limitations and next steps.

**Tags:** Projects, R&D  
**Activation Phrase:** "Guide scientific method for [question]" — Hypothesis + interpretation
