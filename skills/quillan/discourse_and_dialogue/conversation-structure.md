# Conversation Structure

## Overview
Conversation structure examines the organizational patterns that make spoken interaction coherent and meaningful. This covers adjacency pairs (question-answer, greeting-greeting), sequence organization (opening, body, closing), preference organization (preferred vs. dispreferred responses), and overall structural organization of different conversation types (casual chat, service encounter, interview, debate).

## Core Concepts
- **Adjacency Pairs**: Paired utterances where the first part constrains the second (question-answer, offer-acceptance)
- **Sequence Organization**: The ordered phases of an interaction (opening, topic phases, closing)
- **Preference Organization**: Preferred responses are structurally simpler and delivered without delay
- **Insertion Sequences**: Embedded side sequences that postpone the expected second pair part

## Application
Apply conversation structure knowledge for dialogue system design, conversational AI, interview analysis, and understanding natural conversation flow.
