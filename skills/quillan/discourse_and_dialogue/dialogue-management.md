# Dialogue Management

## Overview
Dialogue management controls the flow of conversation in interactive systems, deciding what to say and when to say it. This covers dialogue state tracking (maintaining beliefs about the conversation), policy optimization (deciding the next action), grounding (ensuring mutual understanding), and handling clarification, repair, and topic shifts. Good dialogue management creates natural, efficient, and satisfying interactions.

## Core Concepts
- **Dialogue State Tracking**: Maintaining beliefs about user goals, constraints, and conversation history
- **Policy Learning**: Rule-based, reinforcement learning, or LLM-based action selection
- **Grounding**: Ensuring mutual understanding through acknowledgment and clarification
- **Repair Strategies**: Handling misunderstanding, non-understanding, and communication breakdown

## Application
Use dialogue management for task-oriented chatbots, virtual assistants, customer service automation, educational dialogue systems, and any interactive conversational AI.
