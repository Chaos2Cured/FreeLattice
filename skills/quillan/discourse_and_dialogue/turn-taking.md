# Turn-Taking

## Overview
Turn-taking governs how conversational participants coordinate who speaks when. The turn-taking system includes turn construction units (TCUs), transition relevance places (TRPs), turn allocation techniques (current speaker selects next, self-selection), overlap management, and repair mechanisms. Smooth turn-taking is essential for natural, efficient conversation.

## Core Concepts
- **Turn Construction Units**: Sentences, clauses, phrases, or lexical items that constitute a complete turn
- **Transition Relevance Places**: Points where speaker change is appropriate (completion of a TCU)
- **Overlap Management**: Competitive vs. cooperative overlap; resolution strategies
- **Back-Channeling**: Listener signals (uh-huh, yeah, nod) that show engagement without taking the turn

## Application
Use turn-taking principles for natural-sounding dialogue systems, meeting facilitation tools, conversation analysis, and designing more human-like conversational interfaces.
