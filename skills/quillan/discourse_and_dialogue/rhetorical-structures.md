# Rhetorical Structures

## Overview
Rhetorical structures describe the functional organization of discourse, identifying how parts of a text relate to each other and contribute to the overall communicative goal. Rhetorical Structure Theory (RST) provides a formal framework with relations like Elaboration, Cause, Contrast, Condition, Background, and Purpose, organized in hierarchical trees spanning entire texts.

## Core Concepts
- **Nuclearity**: Distinguishing nucleus (essential) and satellite (supporting) discourse units
- **RST Relations**: Elaboration, Cause, Contrast, Condition, Purpose, Background, Evaluation, and 20+ others
- **Hierarchical Structure**: Discourse units combine recursively into larger structures spanning full texts
- **Relation Signals**: Discourse markers, syntactic constructions, and genre conventions that cue relations

## Application
Apply rhetorical structures for text generation with coherent organization, discourse parsing, argumentation analysis, summarization, and evaluating the logical organization of written texts.
