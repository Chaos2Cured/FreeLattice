# Coherence and Cohesion

## Overview
Coherence and cohesion are the twin pillars of discourse quality. Cohesion refers to the grammatical and lexical links between utterances (reference, substitution, ellipsis, conjunction, lexical cohesion). Coherence refers to the overall logical consistency and meaningfulness of the discourse as a whole. Together they ensure that conversations and texts flow naturally and make sense.

## Core Concepts
- **Cohesive Devices**: Reference (pronouns, demonstratives), substitution, ellipsis, conjunction, lexical cohesion
- **Global Coherence**: Overall topic structure and logical consistency across the entire discourse
- **Local Coherence**: Logical connections between adjacent utterances or sentences
- **Coherence Relations**: Causal, temporal, elaborative, contrastive relations between discourse segments

## Application
Apply coherence and cohesion principles to evaluate and improve generated text, design more natural dialogue, and analyze discourse quality in any textual or conversational context.
