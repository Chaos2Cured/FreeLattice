# Multi-Party Dialogue

## Overview
Multi-party dialogue involves three or more participants interacting conversationally, introducing complexities absent in dyadic conversation. This covers participant roles, address terms, side sequences, interruption management, floor control, and thread management. Multi-party dialogue requires modeling multiple simultaneous relationships and managing competing conversational threads.

## Core Concepts
- **Participation Framework**: Speaker, addressee, side participant, bystander, eavesdropper roles
- **Floor Management**: Who has the conversational floor; how floor is requested, taken, and yielded
- **Thread Management**: Managing multiple simultaneous or interleaved conversational topics
- **Overlap and Interruption**: Competitive vs. cooperative overlap; interruption types and resolutions

## Application
Use multi-party dialogue principles for meeting analysis, group chat systems, multi-agent conversational AI, collaborative tools, and any application involving group communication.
