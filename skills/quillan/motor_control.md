# Motor Control

## Description

The ability to control the movement of a physical or virtual body.

## Components

*   **Kinematics:** The study of the motion of objects without considering the forces that cause them to move.
*   **Dynamics:** The study of the forces that cause objects to move.
*   **Control Theory:** A branch of mathematics that deals with the behavior of dynamical systems.
