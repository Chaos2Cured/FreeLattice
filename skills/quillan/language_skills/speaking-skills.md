# Speaking Skills

## Overview
Speaking skills encompass the ability to produce fluent, coherent, and contextually appropriate spoken language. This involves phonological encoding, lexical access, syntactic formulation, prosodic control, and discourse management in real time. Effective speaking requires balancing accuracy and fluency while adapting to listener needs, social context, and communicative goals.

## Core Concepts
- **Fluency**: Smooth, continuous speech with appropriate pacing and minimal hesitation
- **Pronunciation and Prosody**: Accurate articulation, stress patterns, intonation, and rhythm
- **Discourse Management**: Turn-taking, topic maintenance, repair, and audience adaptation
- **Register and Style**: Adjusting formality, vocabulary, and complexity to context and listener

## Application
Apply speaking skills for presentations, narration, dialogue generation, voice interfaces, language teaching, and any application requiring natural spoken language production.
