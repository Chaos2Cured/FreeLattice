# Grammar and Syntax

## Overview
Grammar and syntax form the structural backbone of language, governing how words combine to form meaningful sentences. This sub-skill covers parts of speech, phrase structure, grammatical relations, agreement phenomena, tense and aspect systems, and syntactic variation across languages. Mastery of grammar enables precise, unambiguous communication and is essential for both language comprehension and generation.

## Core Concepts
- **Parts of Speech**: Nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, determiners
- **Phrase Structure**: Noun phrases, verb phrases, prepositional phrases, and their hierarchical organization
- **Grammatical Relations**: Subject, object, complement, adjunct and how they map to syntactic positions
- **Agreement and Government**: Subject-verb agreement, case government, gender/number concord

## Application
Apply grammar knowledge to parse and generate syntactically correct sentences across registers and dialects. Essential for NLU, NLG, machine translation, and language teaching.
