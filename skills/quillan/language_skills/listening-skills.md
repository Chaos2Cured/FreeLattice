# Listening Skills

## Overview
Listening skills involve the active process of receiving, attending to, and interpreting spoken language. This includes phonetic decoding, word recognition, syntactic parsing of spoken input, discourse tracking, and pragmatic interpretation of prosody and intonation. Effective listening requires managing attention, predicting upcoming content, and integrating spoken information with context and prior knowledge.

## Core Concepts
- **Phonetic Decoding**: Mapping acoustic signals to phonemes, syllables, and words
- **Prosodic Interpretation**: Using intonation, stress, and rhythm to parse meaning and attitude
- **Active Listening**: Attending selectively, predicting, inferring, and monitoring comprehension
- **Note-Taking and Synthesis**: Capturing key information and organizing it coherently

## Application
Apply listening skills for transcription, meeting comprehension, lecture analysis, interview processing, audio content understanding, and real-time spoken interaction.
