# Reading Comprehension

## Overview
Reading comprehension is the ability to extract, interpret, and synthesize meaning from written texts. It involves decoding, literal comprehension, inferential understanding, critical evaluation, and integration with prior knowledge. Effective reading comprehension requires coordinating syntactic parsing, semantic interpretation, discourse processing, and pragmatic inference across texts of varying complexity.

## Core Concepts
- **Literal Comprehension**: Extracting explicitly stated information, facts, and details from text
- **Inferential Comprehension**: Drawing conclusions, identifying implicit causes, predicting outcomes
- **Critical Reading**: Evaluating argument strength, detecting bias, assessing evidence quality
- **Meta-Comprehension**: Monitoring understanding, recognizing confusion, applying repair strategies

## Application
Apply comprehension protocols for deep document analysis, literature review, contract interpretation, research synthesis, and any task requiring thorough understanding of written material.
