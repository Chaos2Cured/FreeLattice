# Vocabulary Building

## Overview
Vocabulary building involves the systematic acquisition, organization, and retrieval of lexical knowledge. This encompasses word meanings, collocations, register-appropriate usage, morphological awareness (roots, affixes), and the mental lexicon's organization. A rich vocabulary enables precise expression, nuanced comprehension, and stylistic flexibility across communicative contexts.

## Core Concepts
- **Morphological Awareness**: Understanding roots, prefixes, suffixes and how they combine to form words
- **Collocations and Constructions**: Words that naturally co-occur and formulaic multi-word expressions
- **Register and Domain**: Vocabulary choices appropriate to formal, informal, technical, or literary contexts
- **Semantic Networks**: How words relate through synonymy, antonymy, hyponymy, and meronymy

## Application
Use vocabulary building strategies to enhance lexical range for NLG, tailor language to audience, understand domain-specific terminology, and improve reading comprehension in specialized fields.
