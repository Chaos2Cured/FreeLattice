# Discourse and Dialogue

## Description

The ability to engage in coherent and meaningful conversations.

## Components

*   **Cohesion:** The grammatical and lexical linking within a text or sentence that holds a text together and gives it meaning.
*   **Coherence:** The quality of being logical and consistent.
*   **Turn-taking:** The process by which people in a conversation decide who is to speak next.
*   **Topic Management:** The ability to introduce, maintain, and change topics in a conversation.
