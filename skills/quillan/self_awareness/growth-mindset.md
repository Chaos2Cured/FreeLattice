# Growth Mindset
## Overview
Growth mindset is the belief that abilities and intelligence can be developed through effort, learning, and persistence. This sub-skill explores the psychological research on fixed versus growth mindsets, their impact on learning and achievement, and practical strategies for cultivating a growth-oriented perspective.
## Core Concepts
- **Fixed vs Growth Mindset**: The distinction between viewing abilities as static versus developable
- **Effort as Strategy**: Reframing effort not as a sign of inadequacy but as the path to mastery
- **Response to Failure**: How mindset shapes reactions to setbacks and challenges
- **Neuroplasticity**: The brains ability to reorganize itself by forming new neural connections throughout life
## Application
Apply growth mindset principles when designing educational systems, building AI that learns from mistakes adaptively, or developing coaching and mentoring frameworks.
