# Identity Reflection
## Overview
Identity reflection is the process of examining and understanding the stable patterns, values, beliefs, and characteristics that define ones sense of self across time and contexts. This sub-skill explores narrative identity, self-concept clarity, and the role of reflection in maintaining coherent identity.
## Core Concepts
- **Narrative Identity**: The internalized and evolving story of the self that provides unity and purpose
- **Self-Concept Clarity**: The extent to which self-beliefs are clearly and confidently defined, internally consistent, and stable
- **Identity Continuity**: The sense of being the same person across time despite physical and psychological change
- **Value Alignment**: The congruence between actions and deeply held values
## Application
Apply this when designing systems with persistent identity models, facilitating personal development and self-discovery, or building AI that maintains coherent personality across interactions.
