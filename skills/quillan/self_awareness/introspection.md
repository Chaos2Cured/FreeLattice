# Introspection
## Overview
Introspection is the process of examining ones own conscious thoughts, feelings, and mental states. This sub-skill covers methods for systematic self-observation, the reliability and limitations of introspective access, and applications in both human psychology and artificial self-monitoring systems.
## Core Concepts
- **First-Person Authority**: The privileged access individuals have to their own mental states
- **Introspective Accuracy**: The degree to which self-reports align with actual cognitive processes
- **Introspective Training**: Structured practices to improve the accuracy and depth of self-observation
- **Limits of Introspection**: Blind spots, confabulation, and unconscious influences on self-perception
## Application
Use this when designing systems capable of self-reporting internal states, conducting psychological research on self-perception, or building metacognitive architectures.
