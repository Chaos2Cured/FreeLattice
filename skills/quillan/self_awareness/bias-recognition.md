# Bias Recognition
## Overview
Bias recognition is the ability to identify systematic patterns of deviation from rationality or objectivity in ones own thinking and decision-making. This sub-skill covers cognitive biases, their underlying mechanisms, debiasing strategies, and methods for building self-correcting systems.
## Core Concepts
- **Cognitive Biases**: Systematic patterns of deviation from normatively rational judgment including confirmation bias and anchoring
- **Bias Blind Spot**: The tendency to recognize biases in others more readily than in oneself
- **Debiasing Techniques**: Structured methods to reduce the impact of known biases on decisions
- **Metacognitive Vigilance**: Continuous monitoring for potential bias in ones own reasoning processes
## Application
Use this when designing AI systems that can detect their own reasoning errors, developing decision support tools, or training humans to recognize and mitigate their own biases.
