3.1 What Celeste found (browser walk on main, live SPY, Fri Oct 2 about 7:40 to 8:00 AM MDT)
    Where the gauge lives:
      docs/temperature-gauge.html            the live gauge (Chart.js 4.4.0 in lib/, zoom plugin)
      docs/temperature-playground.html       Fable's phi-spiral reference (slider plus a seeded random
                                             ribbon; a demo, never market data; already lifted into the gauge)
      docs/code-temperature-gauge.html, docs/code-temperature.html, docs/for-fable-go12-temperature.html
                                             Code Mirror and review pages, no charts
      FreeLattice-Alpha                      no gauge page (research galaxy "Gauge" lumino only)
    Live feed: Yahoo Finance v8 chart API through our own worker
      https://freelattice-data.freelattice.workers.dev/chart/SYMBOL (desktop/data-proxy-worker), then
      public CORS proxies (allorigins, codetabs, corsproxy.org). No demo data on the live page.
    Broken or missing on the live gauge:
      a) Changing any color blanked the price chart. Chart.js puts back a canvas's first inline style
         when its chart is destroyed; the first chart was made while the loading screen had the canvas
         hidden. Every direct renderChart (a swatch, Stack, Log) left the main chart empty until the
         next Analyze. Seen on main with the RSI swatch.
      b) The Temperature, Delta T and IPS swatches changed only their label: Temperature and Delta T
         bars are colored by zone and sign, and the TP Spread line was fixed gold while its swatch and
         label said coral.
      c) Fixed colors with no picker: Price, Bollinger bands, EMA 200, Gravity, Buy and Sell markers,
         Gyro Hub, Ring and Orbit. EMA 8, 12, 24, 50 read a saved color, but nothing in price mode let
         you set one.
      d) Moving averages are EMA only (periods 8, 12, 24, 50, 200, editable). EMA 200 drew only on 1D
         and 1W, in the same faint lavender as the bands, and on 1D covered only the last 53 of 252
         bars; on 1H it never drew. EMA 50 started at bar 50.
      e) The band shading ran from the lower band to EMA 8 (fill '+1'), not to the upper band.
      f) 1W labels were clock times; intraday labels had no day.
      g) 5M failed early in a trading day: "Not enough data" (4 bars at 7:36 AM MDT).
      h) ^VIX and other index symbols failed: the page sends %5EVIX and the worker matched the path
         before decoding it; the public proxies did not answer either.
      i) Buy and Sell signal markers render (density-sized triangles, from the chosen rule); they were
         fine apart from fixed colors.
    Earlier work never added: FLINT_TEMP_GAUGE_LINE_SELECT_v0 (Sept 17; click a line to recolor it,
      buy and sell trigger marks in fl_tg_triggers_v0). No branch, no PR, no marker on main. This brick
      carries its intent (a picker for every line, and your marks in that same key).
