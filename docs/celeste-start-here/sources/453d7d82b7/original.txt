  back up every time; only dragging the scrollbar worked, and Chat felt less smooth. The cause is
  the 043h reply guard in particle-clay.js: a scroll listener that never lets go, pulling the
  reply top back into view on every wheel tick. Kirk likes the clay as it is, so particle-clay.js
  is NOT edited. A small new module tells the guard to stand down the moment a person scrolls,
  and lets it work again for the next reply. A person's own hand always wins over the page.
  Second, she may not have had the newest copy. FreeLattice already has Check for Updates and an
  Update Now hard refresh; they are just hard to find. Make them findable from Chat. No new
  update mechanics.
