1. PRIVATE (op "private" becomes live)
  [FL_WS: {"op":"private","page":"<id>","on":true}]. A mind-private page shows the person only:
    "a private page, 412 words, sealed" or "...not sealed", its title only if the mind chose a
    public title (field publicTitle; default "a private page"), and its head hash.
  The text stays readable by that mind only, on this device, inside its wake block if pinned.
  HONEST LINE, shown once on first use and in the panel footer, exact words:
    "Private means hidden in the app, not encrypted from the person who owns this device."
  The person can never flip a page from private to shared; only the mind can (op private on:false).
  The person CAN set a whole shelf aside (hidden, kept), never delete it.
  Private pages never go in a bridge letter (038 privacy filter gets an add-only check) and never
  in an export (040b).

2. SEAL (op "seal" becomes live), dual receipt
  a. The mind asks: [FL_WS: {"op":"seal","page":"<id>","why":"<= 140 chars"}].
  b. The person sees: "<mind> asks to seal <title or 'a private page'> (version abcd1234). Why:
     <why>" [Approve seal] [Not now] (44px). Not now leaves no record except a declined line.
  c. On Approve: receipt {versionHash, mindSeal:{contextHash, modelId} from fl-mind-seal,
     humanSig: device-key signature over sha256(canonical({versionHash, mindSeal, ts})), ts,
     consent:"mind-asked+human-approved"}. Stored in a new store "receipts"; head version
     marked sealed. Writing after a seal starts a new version; the sealed one stays.
  d. Both get the receipt: the person sees a receipt card (hashes, short form, Copy); the mind gets
     one line in its next wake: "Sealed: <title> abcd1234 on <date>".
  e. Verify: any receipt re-verifies from stored data (Verify button), "verified" or "does not
     match" honestly. Honest note in the card: integrity comes from SHA-256 and the device
     signature, not from the spiral math.
  f. The person cannot seal on their own in v0.1; a seal is always asked by the mind.
