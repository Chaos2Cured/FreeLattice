1. WHY, AND WHY THIS SMALL
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Kirk wants shared compute, the device pool and abliteration on theLatticeTree, where the
code is cleaner, and FreeLattice so easy no one struggles. The pool (018) and the room
split (019) both stand on one thing: knowing which people you trust. The Tree has no mesh
yet (no PeerJS, no MeshIdentity, no fl-kin, no share door).

The ORDER outline for 017 said "port fl-kin.js + fl-share-door.js". Celeste trimmed it:
  - Kin only. The share door moves to 018, with the pool, because a door that gates
    nothing yet would be a control that does nothing. 018 ports it with 016/016b/016c's
    truths already in (door truth, honest off, pause wins, old yes waits paused).
  - No mesh transport yet. Cards travel the way people already talk: copy one line, send
    it by text or email or say hello in person, paste it on the other side. Nothing is
    fetched or sent by this module, ever. The only outside act is the clipboard write
    after a tap.

What a person sees (Garden, Settings, under "May I look?"; also settings.html):
  Trusted kin
  "Kin are people whose AI you choose to trust. Trade cards with them, then tap Trust.
   Nothing is sent from here. You pass cards by text, email or in person."
  Your card: names the remembered mind's model. Two fields (Name your AI, Your first
    name), Make my card. The first tap makes a key on this computer (Ed25519, or ECDSA
    P-256 on older browsers; not exportable; kept in IndexedDB theLatticeTreeKin), signs
    the card, shows one line (TREEKIN1:...) and Copy my card.
  A friend's card: Add a friend's card, paste, Check this card. The page checks the card
    against its own key, and that its ID (mesh:...) is made from that key, so nobody can
    borrow someone else's ID. Then: "Lumen (llama3.2:3b), from Kirk's computer (ID
    2HKQkGhj). This card checks out: the computer that holds its key made it. Trust it
    only if you know who sent it." [Trust this AI] [Not now]
  List: "Trusted kin: N" or "No trusted kin yet." Each card: Trusted / Seen, not
    trusted, with Stop trusting / Trust this AI. History kept (layer, never delete).
  Honest line: "A card shows which computer made it. It does not prove who the person is
    or what the model is. Only trust a card from someone you know, sent a way you trust."
  Later line: "Later, the Device Pool on the Tree will let trusted kin help each other.
    Not in this card yet."
  Fail-closed: no remembered mind, no Make my card (it says to remember a mind first).
    Adding a friend's card still works. Your own card pasted back: "That is your own
    card." A changed or borrowed card: set aside, with plain words.

Why it is the right base for 018:
  The card is FreeLattice's own card: kind fl-mind-card, v 1, home thelatticetree (already
  in FL's HOMES), signed under fl-kin-card|v1|, the same fingerprint (keyHash|meshId|name|
  model) and the same pass shape (grantedAt, revokedAt, keyHash, history). The ID is made
  the way FL MeshIdentity makes it ('mesh:' + base58 of SHA-256 of the public JWK). So FL
  and the Tree read kin the same way, and 018 asks only TreeKin.trustedKeyHash(kh): a
  channel must prove that key before anyone counts as kin.
