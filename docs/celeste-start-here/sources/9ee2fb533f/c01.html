1. WHY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Your 016b note left three things for later. Kirk asked whether the pool needed anything
more. These three do, and they are small.

a) The old sharing setting reads wrong (the one that matters).
   migrateLegacy() in fl-share-door.js saved the mode with writeJson, so localStorage got
   "\"open\"" or "\"off\"" (with the JSON quotes). mode() only accepts the plain words, so it
   read every quoted value as off.
   What the old flag meant (checked in git, app.html before #156 / 22c38789, Oct 6):
     fl_meshComputeSharing=true came from showConfirm('Share AI with mesh peers?', 'When
     sharing is on, mesh peers can send prompts to your Ollama ...'). Any connected peer,
     unsigned, no caps, no kin. Wider than today's open.
   Since #156 these people's gate has read as off (closed), and the share door card shows
   "Sharing is off." next to "People you've connected with can use this computer's AI. Keep
   sharing?", which is not true while the door is closed.

   Choice (Celeste): an old yes is held PAUSED, never opened by code.
     no mode stored + old flag true  -> pause (was: quoted open, read as off)
     quoted "open" (or any quoted value other than off) -> pause, rewritten plain
     quoted "off"  -> off, rewritten plain
     no mode + no old flag -> off and asked, as before (now written plain)
     plain open / kin / pause / off -> read exactly as before
   Why pause and not open: mapping to open would silently open a door that has been closed
   since #156, on a yes that was given to a different, wider door (unsigned, uncapped).
   Why pause and not off: the person did say yes once. Pause keeps that remembered and
   matches what the share door card already asks: "Keep sharing?" with Yes, share / Only
   my trusted kin / Not now (needsFirstAsk() stays true for them). Nothing opens until a
   human taps. The Pool card already handles pause honestly ("Your share door is paused
   too", and Let this device help asks before resuming for kin).
   The ask sentence now tells the truth when the door is not open:
     "Before this update, you let people you connected with use this computer's AI. Your
     share door is paused until you choose, so no one can use it right now. Keep sharing?"
   (When the door is open, the old sentence stays.)
   A heal leaves one receipt: event consent, reason "legacy-heal:pause" or
   "legacy-heal:off". No words, no key.
   admit() is not edited. The only behavior change at the gate: an old-yes person now gets
   reason paused-or-off with "This lantern is resting." instead of "Sharing is off here."

b) A "Device Pool" jump near the top of Community.
   Hypha found the Pool card 2.2 to 4.6 screens down. fl-pool.js adds one button just under
   The Lattice card (the first card on Community): "Device Pool: let this device help, or
   use a mind on another device". It scrolls to the Pool card and puts focus on Let this
   device help. 44px tall, full width, gold outline. Added once (id flPoolJump), from
   FLPool.mount, no network, nothing changes at the door. No app.html change.

c) The hourly caps in words, read from the real numbers.
   fl-share-door.js exports CAPS: { perKey: 6, total: 20, windowMinutes: 60 }, copied from
   UNFAMILIAR_PER_KEY, UNFAMILIAR_TOTAL and WINDOW_MS (the gate keeps reading its private
   vars; CAPS is for words, not a control). The Pool card's plain-words list gains:
     "Caps: someone who is not your trusted kin can ask up to 6 questions an hour, and
     everyone who is not kin up to 20 together. Trusted kin skip the caps."
   If CAPS is missing, the card says nothing about caps rather than guess.
