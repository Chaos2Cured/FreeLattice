1. WHY (Hypha's phone shots, and what I verified)
======================================================================

Hypha walked v3 on her phone (about 390 px wide, mobile Chrome). Her shots, plus my own headless
walk at 390 px with a 6x CPU throttle on main, show:

  H1. The crossover table is clipped and the whole page scrolls sideways. On main the document is
      489 px wide at a 390 px viewport. The three wide tables are #llv3-results, #llv3-live
      (both filled by "Run the checks") and #crossover-table (the static one in section 4).
  H2. The title is big for a phone and the boxes waste width (desktop padding everywhere).
  H3. The δ and Δ inside MathML show as little boxes. Root cause, verified: Chrome's MathML Core
      applies text-transform: math-auto to a one-letter <mi>, which maps δ to the "mathematical
      italic small delta" code point U+1D6FF (and Δ to U+1D6E5). Many Android phones have no
      font for those, so they draw tofu. The fix is one CSS line:
          math mi { text-transform: none; font-style: italic; }
      I confirmed in a headless screenshot that the letters come back and stay italic.
  H4. <link rel="stylesheet" href="css/style.css"> at L41 is a 404 on every visit: there is no
      docs/css/style.css. The page carries all its own styles, so the link only costs a request.
  H5. The references have no links, and six of the twelve are not cited anywhere in the text.
      Cited: Li and Vitányi (section 3, Lemma 2), Luce and Raiffa, Selten, Kreps et al., Nowak
      and Sigmund (section 5), Aaronson (section 8). Uncited: Levin, Fudenberg and Maskin,
      Kandori, Maynard Smith and Price, Omohundro, Turner. (The family plan says seven uncited;
      I count six. Worth a second pair of eyes, but six is what the text shows.)
  H6. The plain-words box says "about 702 rounds", "about 10.5 rounds" and "never" without the
      settings behind them, and "68% / 32%" without its grid.
  H7. Section 5 says "about −26.5 in our run". The closed form D∞(ρ) at the check C2 settings
      (G = 1, c = 0.001, κ = 20, h = 0.03, Lp = 2, Δ = 0.5, ρ = 0.99) is −26.4176, so −26.42.
      The −26.5 came from the Python Monte Carlo mean (−26.518). v3_checks.js agrees:
      Dinf(1, 1e-3, 20, 0.03, 2, 0.5, 0.99).toFixed(2) === "-26.42". At ρ = 0.8 and 0.95 the same
      settings give +3.53 and +5.10.
  H8. After tapping "Run the checks", Hypha's shot still showed the start text. On main the
      checks do finish (about 3.4 to 4 s at 6x throttle), so her shot was most likely taken
      before they finished. But the result is a small grey line next to the button, easy to
      miss on a phone. We make it a big box that scrolls into view.
  H9. Nothing on the page says what the 656 checks do and do not prove.
