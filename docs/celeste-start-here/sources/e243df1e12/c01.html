----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The Tree's cards (Tree Glow 1 and 2a, garden-rooms.css) speak one language: Garden night,
a gold breath, an emerald hush, soft rings of light, words you can read. FreeLattice's card
rooms are flat (rgba 0.04 boxes) and their one-line descriptions sit at 0.4 alpha.
renderCardGrid draws every card with inline styles and no classes, so this layer finds the
cards by place (grid children) and adds light without changing the renderer.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IT ADDS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  docs/modules/fl-card-glow.css   new, 71 lines. Scoped only to #more-hub-grid, #learn-hub-grid
                                  and #tab-community's three static cards (The Lattice, Connect
                                  with someone, Shared Knowledge).
     - gold breath at the top and emerald hush at the foot (background-image only, so each card
       keeps its own color; a loved card stays gold)
     - inset top light and a soft lift shadow; on hover a gold ring and glow (also on
       focus-within, ready for when cards become keyboard reachable; today they are not)
     - icons breathe a small gold light
     - descriptions 0.4 -> 0.68, subtitles -> 0.66, the "?" mark 0.25 -> 0.6, help words 0.86
       (all clear 4.5:1 on the night page; the smoke computes it)
     - no transform, no z-index, no isolation, so a help note is never hidden under the next card
     - prefers-reduced-motion: no transition
  index.html + docs/app.html      one <link rel="stylesheet" href="modules/fl-card-glow.css">
                                  after the fl-pool.js script, before </head>. Same in both.
  docs/scripts/smoke-fl-card-glow-v0.js
Not touched: renderCardGrid, PLAY_CARDS, the Quiet Room card, Research (lighthouse) cards,
Settings, sw.js, docs/sw.js, fl-connect.js. A few !important rules lift only text colors and
the glow image over inline styles; the inline styles stay as written.
