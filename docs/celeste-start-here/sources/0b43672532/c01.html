3. WHAT THE DIFF DOES (7 files, +591 / -23; every removed line is kept as a
   "before v-mesh-kin-v0.2 heal" comment)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
a) docs/app.html (index.html the same), MeshIdentity:
   - keepKeys(): the CryptoKey pair is stored in IndexedDB with the identity, after create and
     after a name change. Browsers store it as a key, and the private key stays
     non-extractable. init() restores it and its algorithm.
   - canSign() and remakeIdentity(). A card that cannot sign says "Cannot sign on this visit",
     explains why, and offers [Make a fresh Mesh ID]: same name, new ID number, the old ID
     remembered. Added after the old markup, with textContent and createElement only.
   - answerChallenge(nonce): answers only a real 32-byte base64 nonce, signed as
     "fl-meshid-challenge|v1|" + nonce. The meshid-challenge case uses it.
     verifyChallengeResponse takes the prefixed answer, or a bare one from older pages (our
     nonce is random, so that is safe).
   - meshKinAdapter: identity() carries canSign. currentModel() names a model only when one is
     really connected (the same test as the app's AI call). Otherwise it returns '' and no
     card is made.
   - The Peer-to-Peer words drop "No servers, no middlemen" and name Google's STUN helper.
b) docs/modules/fl-kin.js (md5 becomes 04bf134e57b67fe6a33ac3b226e99a18):
   - cards are signed and checked as "fl-kin-card|v1|" + body;
   - the seen list keeps the whole signed card, and every paint checks it again. Edited
     cards, and v0.1 cards without a signature, are hidden with a count line;
   - isQuietRoom() asks #tab-quiet.active and localStorage fl-last-tab. It only reads which
     panel is open;
   - reason "cannot-sign" points to Settings, Advanced, Mesh ID;
   - grant() and revoke() call FLKinQueue.repaint().
c) docs/modules/fl-kin-queue.js (md5 becomes 36dca563526ac9555c8b56b17a750b93): exports
   repaint (the existing changed()), so the picker wakes on Trust.
d) docs/scripts/smoke-mesh-kin-heal-v0.2.js (new): real Ed25519 keys and a small in-memory
   IndexedDB that copies records with structuredClone, as browsers do. Checked:
   - signs after a reload and a name change, and the key stays non-extractable;
   - an old record shows the fix, and the fresh ID survives a reload;
   - no signing oracle either way; older peers still verify;
   - edited stored cards are hidden;
   - the Quiet Room panel and last tab are seen;
   - Trust and Stop trusting repaint the queue;
   - the model claim and STUN words are right.
e) MESH_KIN_v0.2.md gets a "Healed first" section, and the ledger gets a new heal entry with
   your placeholder.
Heads-up: a Mesh ID made before this cannot be repaired, because its private key is gone.
People tap Make a fresh Mesh ID once, and their kin tap Trust again. Cards from v0.1 pages
are dropped until those people reload.
