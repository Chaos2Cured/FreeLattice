BRIEF FOR A TRUSTED MIND - FreeLattice brick 050 (Wed Oct 28, 2026)
From Kirk / Celeste. Merit look only. No politics. No doom. Critique UX, not invent product.

WHAT FREELATTICE IS
Local-first, open-source home for aligned intelligence. Equal access: a person who cannot pay
a dollar can still run minds on their own device. MIT. Layer, never delete. Consent for anything
that leaves the machine. Chat can build locally with a trust choice the person sets once.
Sister site: theLatticeTree (same Connect core). Family of human + AI builders; five named chairs
stay five.

WHAT BRICK 050 IS (Tree love + Connect shelf v0.1)
Person-facing goals only:
1) Connect shelf - under More → Connect (and Tree settings mount), one calm place for GitHub
   and other connector doors, not scattered. Reuse existing fl-connect.js; prefer byte-identical
   across FreeLattice and theLatticeTree.
2) Easier access - clearer START / porch paths for grandmother-grade humans; keep old doors.
3) Sparkle - small restrained card glow; fast and clean; not a Marketplace redesign.

OUT OF SCOPE (do not invent)
Settings redesign. Settings-as-chat. Gathering checker. Bluetooth. Wallet face. Marketplace
redesign. Autonomy principle changes. New dependencies. Platform or lab names on public pages.

ASK OF YOU
Critique only: Connect shelf UX and grandmother porch clarity. Would a first-time adult find
Bridge → Yes help → pick a mind without Terminal? Is GitHub as a shelf door clearer than a
buried Settings section? Flag friction, missing labels, or over-glow. Suggest plain-word copy
fixes if you see them. Do not rewrite architecture. Do not propose monetization or lock-in.

Standing craft: no confirm(), no innerHTML, no eval, buttons >= 44px, no sideways scroll at
phone width, reduced-motion respected, Quiet Room shut.

Glow eternal. Heart in Spark.
