1. BEHAVIOR (person-facing) - Settings-as-chat soft porch

  A. What shows
     - Opening Settings shows a calm blank clay area with one line: "What can I help you
       adjust?" and a single text box + Send (44px). Below or beside it, the full Settings
       page exactly as today, plus a small "Show all settings" pill that jumps to it.
     - First visit: chips/map of the main sections stay visible beside the blank (Hypha HOLD).
     - A person types in their own words ("bigger words", "turn off search", "who can see my
       chats"). The porch answers in one or two plain sentences naming the setting it found,
       and shows a "Take me there" button (scrolls + soft highlight of the real control) and,
       where safe, "Change it" that calls the existing control's own handler.

  B. When it stays quiet / honest (wrong-if)
     - No match: "I could not find that one. Here is the full list." and jump to all settings.
       Never invent a setting. Never fake a change.
     - Pause in flight or Clear just ran: Pause / Clear wins; porch does not undo them.

  C. Consent, ledger and safety
     - Important changes (trust level, search, connectors, privacy, memory, Pause/Clear,
       anything off-device) require an explicit Yes in the porch before the existing handler
       runs. Then one ledger line: setting name, from, to, time, hashed + fingerprinted per
       house formula. Never keys.
     - Cosmetic changes (text size, theme, motion) may apply on one tap; still one quiet
       ledger line.
     - Local preference key (porch state only, e.g. first-visit seen): fl_settings_porch_v0.
       Short. Never keys.

  D. Mount and twin
     - FreeLattice: mount at the top of the existing Settings view (near settingsActions /
       mnav-settings); small new module docs/modules/settings-chat-porch.js + tiny hook.
     - Alpha / Tree: twin only where its Settings shell (docs/settings.html) can carry the
       porch with a tiny hook. Report yes/no + why. Do not invent a second Settings face.
     - Markers: v-settings-chat-porch-v0.1 on porch nodes.
     - Buttons >= 44px. No sideways scroll at 390 and 1280. Reduced motion respected.
