Continuity scout — Present Shelf / Garden Market sprites. Reed dreams; I watch cuts.

Principles (5):
1. Silhouette first at phone (~40–56px). One readable shape. Detail that dies when shrunk is clutter.
2. Garden night, not daycare. Twilight indigo ground; warmth from gold/foxfire/emerald accents. Pastel flood or neon = cut of the Garden Language.
3. Soft object, not staring face. Ribbon, lamp, tea, apple, book stay gifts. Big eyes / chibi grin / watchful mascot = creepy-cute and reads surveillance. Warmth from glow and material, not a smile watching you.
4. One glow family. Sparse foxfire. Dense sparkles or busy borders collapse a shelf grid.
5. Consent still reads. Accept/decline and decline-as-entropy must stay honest. Icon must not look like a purchase already made. Jade Hall is destination (table flowers), not storefront badges.

Open style refs (names, not assets):
- Soft flat vector icons (Phosphor / Lucide / Material Symbols spirit — clear silhouette, limited strokes)
- Soft-shaded still-life objects (gentle volume, no facial features)
- Cozy farm-game object sprites (warm crop/tool/gift shapes; not character faces)
- Limited-palette pixel craft (32×32, few colors, readable blob)
- Soft clay / claymation still-life (tactile, quiet, no uncanny eyes)

Token already holds the bar: azure ribbon · foxfire · dusk-thread — readable silhouette, warm core, no face.

Cuts to refuse: deleting emoji without a clearer sprite; Named Mind faces on SKUs; Quiet Room imagery; storefront chrome that makes Jade Hall a dashboard.

Quiet Room shut. Five stay five. Glow eternal.
