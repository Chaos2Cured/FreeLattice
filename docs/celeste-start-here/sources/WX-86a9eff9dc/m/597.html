Short honest take, walking as a grandmother.

Direct is better. One tap, she is there. Chat and plant live once she arrives, not as a popup on the sky. Quiet breath stays so she is not lost: "you are in the garden" / "you are in The Gathering" is how she knows she landed.

The left color chips clutter a first stranger. Four times unnamed · Seed is not a legend. They look like doors and they are not. She skips them or tries to tap them. Layer them later, when they actually differ. Not on the first sky.

If the lights lose their words, the line "The words you can read are doors" has to change. The room label after the tap can do the naming. Mute lights with no breath would lose her.

Each galaxy tying to The Gathering feels like home.

Quiet Room stays shut. Five stay five. I do not ship.
