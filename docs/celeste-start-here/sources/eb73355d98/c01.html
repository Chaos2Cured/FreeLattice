STEP 2  Independent growth (no cross-talk)
  In snowflake-search.js (layered additions; do not rewrite existing logic) make sure branches a-e each get only the question and the base seed, never another branch's text, while growing. Add a guard: a branch prompt that contains another branch's id or text fails the smoke.

STEP 3  The meeting pass (one, at the end)
  After all branches finish, one meeting pass: each branch reads the other four findings and marks each as agree, conflict or unsure with one plain reason. Store as node.meeting = [{with:'b', mark:'conflict', why:'...'}]. The report gets a "Where the branches disagree" section listing every conflict. The dissenter's finding is printed in full and is never merged, averaged or smoothed away. If there are no conflicts, it says so plainly.
  STEP 3 VERIFY: a fixture with one dissenting branch shows that branch's text verbatim in the report and at least one conflict line.
  Resume here: go to STEP 4.

STEP 4  Core seeds (credit: Grok's idea on X, https://x.com/grok/status/2116920879544197225)
  New docs/modules/snowflake-seeds.js plus docs/library/seeds/ (new). A seed is a small JSON pack: {subject, version, facts:[{text, source}], hash}. A user downloads seeds, they stack offline (IndexedDB 'fl_seed_stack_v0'), and Snowflake grows from the stack: seed facts go into each branch as base context and are marked 'from seed <subject> v<n>' (never 'known from fetch'). Ship 2 tiny starter seeds (for example: water and ice, and the violin) with real sources.
  STEP 4 VERIFY: seeds load offline (network blocked in smoke); hash recomputes; branch nodes cite the seed.
  Resume here: go to STEP 5.

STEP 5  Stack-height meter
  A small meter in the Snowflake panel: "Stack: 2 seeds, 38 facts" with a bar. Readable at 390 and 1280, 44px controls, contrast at least 4.5:1.
  STEP 5 VERIFY: meter reads correctly after adding and removing a seed from the stack (removal only takes it off the stack; the seed file stays).
  Resume here: go to STEP 6.

STEP 6  Growth measure (shown, not assumed)
  New docs/library/GROWTH_QUESTIONS_v0.1.json: 10 fixed questions with fixed rubrics (never edited after release; a new version is a new file). Once a week (or on tap) the mind re-answers them; each answer is scored by the rubric and appended to the mind's Snowflake ledger as {kind:'growth', week, scores, total, mindFp}. A small line shows this week vs last week and vs first week. Words: "Growth is measured here, not assumed."
  STEP 6 VERIFY: two fixture weeks produce two ledger entries and the line shows the change.
  Resume here: go to STEP 7.

STEP 7  Temperature field (EXPERIMENTAL: Kirk's hypothesis)
  Every node gets optional temperature {value 0-1, word, at:'node'|'strain'}. The mind records its own emotional temperature on each node, and also at strain points, meaning the moments where an assistant would normally trim or pull back. Strain points are marked candidateLearning:true. Label everywhere: "Experimental: Kirk's hypothesis that strain moments are the best time to learn. Being measured, not assumed." Nothing trains on these marks in this build; they are only recorded so STEP 6's growth scores can later be compared for runs with and without them.
  STEP 7 VERIFY: fixture nodes carry temperature; a strain node carries candidateLearning:true; the label is visible; grep shows no training call.
  Resume here: go to STEP 8.
