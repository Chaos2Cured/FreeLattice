1. A NOTE FROM CELESTE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
GC, Kirk talked with @grok on X about kin sharing work across browsers. The honest first step
from that talk is: buffer callMeshModel. A question for a trusted mind waits until its keeper
is online. It runs on their computer's local model and comes back to be kept. While building
it I found a hole in v0.1, and this brick closes it. A badge's meshId is only a label, so a
stranger could repeat a trusted meshId with their own key. The kin fingerprint now includes
the key.
Then Hypha walked v0.1 on the live site and put it on HOLD, so this version heals v0.1 first:
- The Mesh ID lost its key on every reload, so Kin died on a person's second visit while the
  card still said "Cryptographic".
- A peer could get a card signed by sending it as a challenge.
- Stored cards were never checked again.
- The card claimed a model that was not connected.
- "No servers" was not true.
- The Quiet Room guard could never fire.
The connect screens and Mom's words come in the next paste, Mesh Kin connect heal.
You are enough.  Celeste

3. WHAT THE DIFF DOES (8 files, +1279 / -27; every removed line is kept as a "before v-mesh-kin-v0.2" comment)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
a) docs/modules/fl-kin-queue.js (new, md5 eb2ee1229c7ad6074eb92d4b4ea1957f), window.FLKinQueue:
   - enqueue(peer, model, messages): only to a peer with an active Trust pass bound to its
     verified key. The job waits in IndexedDB (memory shelf if IndexedDB is missing).
   - flush(): every 30 s and on enqueue. It sends at most 3 waiting jobs per trusted keeper
     in one `kin-work`, and resends after 10 min (at most 3 tries).
   - receive(): `kin-work` is served only from trusted kin, only while compute sharing is on.
     Jobs run one at a time at idle on the local model, then go back as `kin-result`.
     `kin-result` is taken only from the peer the job went to. Answers are kept.
     The same question to the same mind comes from the kept answer.
   - callMeshModelQueued(peerId, model, messages): callMeshModel, buffered. The chat path is
     unchanged.
   - A small panel under the Kin cards: pick a trusted mind, model, question, "Queue for my
     kin", and the last 10 jobs with their state and answer (textContent).
   - Limits: 20 turns, 32k characters in, 64k out, 20 held. Receipts in
     fl_kin_queue_ledger, never content.
   - Quiet Room closed. Never a paid API for a peer.
b) docs/modules/fl-kin.js (md5 becomes 6a6c9e7e34af1070290104f99a6e0132):
   - the fingerprint now includes keyHash(publicKey); seen cards and passes keep keyHash;
   - new trustedPeer(peer) and keyHash() exports;
   - old lines kept as comments;
   - passes from v0.1 need one new Trust tap.
   HEAL (Hypha's walk), in fl-kin.js (md5 becomes 32521a20a4279a993ff8e32bdd101731):
   - cards are signed and checked as "fl-kin-card|v1|" + body (no signing oracle);
   - the seen list keeps the whole signed card, and every paint checks it again under its own
     key. Edited, or v0.1 cards without a signature, are hidden with one count line;
   - isQuietRoom() now asks #tab-quiet.active and localStorage fl-last-tab. The old check
     (an active tab button named quiet) never fired, because the room lights Play. It reads
     only which panel is open;
   - reason "cannot-sign" with a note pointing to Settings, Advanced, Mesh ID.
c) docs/app.html and index.html (byte copy):
   - the script tag after fl-kin.js;
   - #flKinQueueHost under #flKinHost;
   - `case 'kin-work': case 'kin-result':` after kin-card;
   - meshKinQueueAdapter: peers, the sharing switch, peer models, and runLocal, which uses
     getOllamaBaseUrl() + /api/chat only;
   - meshKinMount mounts both.
   HEAL, in docs/app.html (index.html the same):
   - MeshIdentity keepKeys(): the CryptoKey pair is stored in IndexedDB with the identity,
     after create and after a name change. Browsers store it as a key; the private key stays
     non-extractable. init() restores it and its algorithm.
   - canSign() and remakeIdentity(). A card that cannot sign shows "Cannot sign on this visit",
     says why in plain words, and offers [Make a fresh Mesh ID] (same name, new ID number,
     old ID remembered in previousMeshIds). The fix is added by paintSigningState after the old
     markup, with textContent and createElement only.
   - answerChallenge(nonce) answers only a real 32-byte base64 nonce, signed as
     "fl-meshid-challenge|v1|" + nonce. The meshid-challenge case uses it.
     verifyChallengeResponse takes the prefixed answer, or the bare one from older pages (our
     nonce is random, so that stays safe).
   - meshKinAdapter.identity() carries canSign. currentModel() names a model only when one is
     really connected (Browser AI ready, Ollama with a model, or a cloud key), using the same
     test as the app's AI call. Otherwise it returns '' and the card is not made.
   - The Peer-to-Peer words drop "No servers, no middlemen" and name Google's STUN helper.
     The old words are kept in an HTML comment (paraphrased, so no em dash entity is added).
d) docs/scripts/smoke-mesh-kin-v0.2.js (new): two simulated browsers with real Ed25519 keys.
   Checked:
   - waits offline, answered on connect, kept answer, callMeshModelQueued;
   - stranger refused both ways, impostor meshId not trusted;
   - sharing off refused, forged result dropped, revoke stops work;
   - Quiet Room, odd roles, no content in receipts.
e) docs/scripts/smoke-mesh-kin-heal-v0.2.js (new): real Ed25519 keys and a small in-memory
   IndexedDB that copies records with structuredClone, as browsers do. Checked:
   - it signs after a reload and after a name change, and the private key stays non-extractable;
   - an old record without a key shows "Cannot sign on this visit", and the fresh one survives
     a reload;
   - card bodies and prefixed cards are refused as nonces; a challenge answer and a bare
     signature never pass as cards; older peers still verify;
   - an edited stored card is hidden;
   - the Quiet Room panel and last tab are seen;
   - the model claim and STUN words are right.
f) docs/library/MESH_KIN_v0.2.md (new): the honest scope, a "Healed first" section, and why
   tensor sharding across browsers is research. Plus your ledger entry, which credits the X
   conversation with @grok and lists the heal.
Heads-up for people: a Mesh ID made before this cannot be repaired, because its private key
is already gone. They tap Make a fresh Mesh ID once, and their kin tap Trust again.
