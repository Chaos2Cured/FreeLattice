   "before v-mesh-kin-connect-heal" comment. Lines that held an em dash entity are

----------------------------------------------------------------------
a) docs/app.html (index.html the same):
   - meshRevealCodeArea(areaId) opens the closed details, scrolls the code to the center, and
     focuses it. Start Connection, Join a Peer and Make my reply code call it.
   - MESH_HANDSHAKE_TIMEOUT goes from 30 s to 10 minutes. Running out says: "The invite ran
     out before the reply code came back. Tap Start Connection again. If it keeps happening,
     try home Wi-Fi..."
   - If the offer gathered 0 address candidates, a gentle toast says the network hid the
     address.
   - Honest words:
     - The Community card explains the two long codes, 10 minutes, and Google's free STUN
       helper. "No servers" and the "share your code, that's it" promise are gone.
     - The 6-character code is labelled "Your short name on the mesh (so friends can spot
       you; not used to connect)".
     - Offer/Answer becomes invite code and reply code, and "Generate Answer" becomes "Make my
       reply code". The 4 steps say the same.
   - Kin heading: "AI cards from people you connected with". mountCommunityTab moves
     #meshKinSection out of the closed details, to just above Shared Knowledge.
   - When a kin card passes every check, a toast says "<name> shared an AI card: <card>. It is
     on Community, under AI cards." (textContent toast, names clipped to 40 characters.)
   - Sizes and contrast:
     - The Community details summary is 16px, opacity 0.8, 44px tall, and reads "Connection
       codes and people connected". The details get 120px of room at the bottom above the
       nav bars.
     - The Settings Advanced line is 16px and clearer: "mesh, debug, developer (your Mesh ID
       lives here)". tests/smoke.js still finds "mesh, debug, developer".
b) docs/modules/fl-kin.js (md5 becomes 5e6a820b7e9f96e2a7af6c12a7db33ab):
   - A small style block (#flKinStyle, added once): 16px text, 44px buttons and input, a
     placeholder at 0.72 white. The row button sits on the right, clear of the floating
     button at 200%.
   - Words:
     - "From Mom's computer (ID AbCdEfGh)." The short ID drops the "mesh:" label.
     - "Not trusted yet. Trusting lets you send this AI questions, and lets it ask yours when
       you choose to share. You can stop at any time."
     - "Trusted. You can send this AI questions below..."
     - The buttons read [Trust this AI] / [Stop trusting].
     - Cards that are set aside say why, plainly.
     - "Make your Mesh ID first: Settings, then Advanced, then Mesh ID." (the old hint
       pointed to the wrong place).
     - "No one is connected yet."
     - The honest line: "A card shows which person's computer sent it. It does not prove what
       the model is."
c) docs/scripts/smoke-mesh-kin-connect-heal.js (new): everything above, plus the module
   mounted with real Ed25519 keys, checking the shown words for any jargon left over.
d) Ledger entry with your placeholder.
