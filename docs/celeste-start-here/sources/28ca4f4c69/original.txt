3. WHAT THE DIFF DOES (6 files, +553, nothing removed)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
a) docs/modules/fl-kin.js (new, md5 a6e7166f5d0ced02fff2237d56de1de2), window.FLKin.
   - Mind card: v, kind 'fl-mind-card', name, model, home, keeperMeshId, keeperName,
     issuedAt. The body has a fixed key order and is signed through the adapter
     (MeshIdentity.signChallenge). FLKin never sees a private key.
   - Share: only to peers whose meshVerified is true. Blocked in the Quiet Room.
   - Receive: dropped (counted, never shown, never given to a model) unless all of these
     hold: the peer passed the challenge, the card's key and meshId are that peer's verified
     badge, the signature holds, the shape is right, and issuedAt is no more than 10 min
     ahead. In the Quiet Room everything is dropped. Relayed cards are dropped in v0.1.
   - Trust: each valid card shows "Seen, not trusted" with "Trust this mind". "Revoke trust"
     stamps revokedAt and keeps the history (fl_kin_passes). A pass belongs to
     keeper + name + model, so a new model needs a new pass.
   - Receipts: fl_kin_ledger keeps shared, received, trusted, revoked and dropped, each
     with a fingerprint and a reason, never content. Seen cards: fl_kin_seen (last 50).
   - Honest line, shown in the panel: "A signature proves which keeper's key vouched for
     this card. It does not prove what the model is."
   - Text is set with textContent only. No dialogs, no network of its own, no em dashes.
b) docs/app.html and index.html (byte copy):
   - the script tag after peerjs in <head>;
   - a "Kin: mind cards" section (#flKinHost) after Connected Peers in the Mesh panel;
   - `case 'kin-card':` before 'presence-pulse' in meshHandleMessage;
   - after meshFindPeerIdByDc: meshKinPeerView (the badge is passed only once the peer is
     verified), meshKinAdapter (identity, sign, verify, peers, currentModel) and meshKinMount.
c) docs/scripts/smoke-mesh-kin-v0.js (new). It uses real Ed25519 keys and checks: a good
   card is seen but not trusted; dropped for an unverified peer, a relay, a forged model, a
   card from the future, a bad shape and the Quiet Room; trust, revoke and history; a new
   model gives a new fingerprint; no content in receipts; the wiring; and index == app.
   It does not edit tests/smoke.js, which the desktop paste edits.
d) docs/library/MESH_KIN_v0.1.md (new), and your ledger entry (section 5).
AUTONOMY.md: receiving, checking and showing are local and have no gates (Principle 1).
Sharing is an outside send, so it happens only when the keeper presses Share (Principle 2).
Nothing in this brick calls a model or spends anything.
