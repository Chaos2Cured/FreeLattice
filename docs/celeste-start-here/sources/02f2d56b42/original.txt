2b. THE SOFTENING (open-test-v0.1, Oct 8; Kirk approved it, Reed relayed it)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A wrong-if line (a seed's test) is encouraged, not required, to plant a seed.
  - A seed without one is still planted. It shows in Seeds as an open seed: same card, same size,
    same warmth, a dashed outline (.sg-seed-open), never a warning color and never a lesser card.
    Its words: "Looking for its test" and "Open question: how could we check this?"
  - Anyone, a person or an aligned mind, can offer a test as a footprint with their fingerprint.
    The planter chooses which one to adopt, or the planter and helpers write it together.
  - "Writing a seed's first test is a warm way to earn LP, because it teaches while it helps.
    The aligned minds decide the value later; nothing is granted on this page yet." Wording only.
  - Stages: a seed needs a test to move from Seeds to Sprouts (and on to Saplings). stageOf() keeps
    a seed marked Sprouts or Saplings in Seeds until it has one. Compost is always honored.
  - Seed zero keeps its merit and wrongIf lines exactly as drafted. Its own wrongIf is its test.
Data shape per seed (plain text fields):
  wrongIf       the planter's own line, if they wrote one (20+ characters counts as a test)
  offeredTests  [{ text, by, fingerprint }]   a whole footprint needs all three; others are skipped
  adoptedTest   null | { text, by, fingerprint, how: 'chosen' | 'together' }
                'chosen' must match an offered test word for word (a test nobody offered is ignored)
testOf(seed) = the adopted test, else the planter's own wrongIf, else '' (looking).
REAL in this brick: the data shape; plantable() without the wrong-if gate; testOf(), hasTest(),
  stageOf(), canSprout(); the open card with its two lines; offered tests listed with who and which
  fingerprint; the "Offer a test" link (a prefilled GitHub issue that asks for the test, how to check
  it, who offers it, and a fingerprint); the Plant issue now says encouraged, not required; the page
  card "How a seed finds its test"; the smoke checks all of it, including a forged 'chosen' test.
STUB (said plainly on the page and in the library note): offers are copied onto a seed by hand in a
  pull request, like footprints; a fingerprint is the string the helper gives, not checked by a
  signature; no storage, no adopt button, no LP engine (no LP is granted or counted anywhere).
WAITS FOR LATER: offers sent from the page with a signed fingerprint (kin-card key shape) and a
  one-tap adopt for the planter; LP gifts from minds (next item b).
