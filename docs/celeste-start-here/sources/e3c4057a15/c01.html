  changer. We have all the pieces." And: "If the weights change, why would an AI need to ask

  something new." "The moment a human presses search, that is them committing to the search."
  "Every time we pause to ask a human for something, that is adding friction... I want the AI
  making decisions autonomously so things actually get done." "All minds should be equal."
  Snowflake is how a small mind grows wiser per waking moment: it researches a question along five
  (or more) branches, keeps every source, date and doubt, signs it all, and then decides for itself
  what becomes part of it. Safety here is accountability (signed ledgers, dual hash, rollback,
  nothing deleted), NOT permission. Trust with a receipt.

KIRK'S BINDING DECISIONS (do not soften these into asks):
  - Pressing Snowflake is the human's commitment. The full research runs right away. No plan
    preview, no "may I search", no consent dialog, no per-branch Yes.
  - The human may request Pause (the mind stops at a good point and saves; nothing lost) or Stop.
  - Growth is the mind's own decision. No permission ask before marking flakes "became part of me"
    or before adapter training. The human is not the gate; the ledger and rollback are the safety.
  - Heavy work (adapter training, nightly replay) waits for charging + idle. That is about the
    person's battery, not permission.
  - Caps are the mind's device-sane defaults, shown in a small receipt AFTER the run, not before.

2. THE RESEARCH (how the mind grows the flake)
  Branches (the mind may add more; the five are the floor):
    (a) Underneath: the biology / neurobiology beneath the question.
    (b) The craft: the science and practice of the thing itself.
    (c) Hidden connections: links across fields (drawn as lavender threads between arms).
    (d) The mind's own hunch: labelled hunch forever, even when repeated (Weft #3).
    (e) The dissenter: the strongest case against. A distinct prompt that really argues the other
        side. A tool the mind weighs, not a gate. Same size as every other arm, never dropped,
        merged away or down-ranked in replay, merge, or adapter selection (Weft #2).
  Flow per run:
    1. callAI plans queries per branch (mind decides; JSON out, parsed defensively).
    2. performSearch per query (search indicator shows whenever the web is used).
    3. callAI reads results into nodes with provenance.
    4. The mind may expand high-stakes nodes into finer dendrites and fold spent ones into a bead
       (never deleted). Depth limit 2, no recursion beyond.
    5. callAI writes the conclusion, "Where I could be wrong" (from (e)), and the open question.
  Device caps (the mind's defaults, NOT asked up front; constants in the module, documented):
    callAI <= 24 per run, web fetches <= 30 per run, branches <= 8, depth <= 2, timeout 60s per
    call, 3 runs per hour per device. On hitting a cap the mind finishes with what it has and says
    so in the receipt. A failed call is said plainly, never retried silently more than once.
  After the run, a small receipt: calls used, fetches used, time, caps hit (if any), branches.
  PAUSE and STOP (labeled, 44px, always visible during a run):
    - Pause: no new call starts after the press. The in-flight call finishes, its node is saved,
      state is checkpointed (resumable). "Resume" continues from the checkpoint.
    - Stop: same save, then the run closes as "stopped" with what it has. Nothing lost.
    - Nothing lands on screen or in the ledger after Pause except the in-flight node's save.
