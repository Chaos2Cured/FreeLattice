----------------------------------------------------------------------

    mDNS in phone browsers). The old link flow heals the record. Later idea: a DHCP
    reservation note in the help words.
  - Reconnect skips the Mac window for remembered devices. That is the whole point, and why
    Forget this device must be one tap and Pause must always win.
  - Keep-on means the Mac answers on the LAN for as long as the app runs. It still only
    serves /relay, /challenge and /knock, and still trusts no stranger.
  - The relay tab still opens for a moment on the phone (same as 033c).
