1. WHAT HYPHA SAW, AND THE EXACT CAUSE
======================================================================
Seen (thelatticetree.com/settings.html, 2026-09-30): on load the page checks 12 local ports and
shows "Looking… next look in 8s". The grandmother card on the same page says "May I look? —
only when you ask" and "We only look at well-known local doors, and only when you ask."

Cause (Alpha main 25e6ded, fl-connect.js blob 2e02eae3, same bytes as FreeLattice):
  a) docs/settings.html, final inline script, runs on load:
       if (connect && window.FlConnect && typeof FlConnect.mount === 'function') FlConnect.mount(connect);
     The garden veil does the same when Settings opens (garden-rooms.js ~1716, FlConnect.mount(connectHost)).
  b) fl-connect.js mount(el) ends with lookAgain(), which resets the loop and calls
     refreshUI({ force: true }) -> probe({ gesture: true }).
  c) probe(): no saved port, so the first tick does the full Bridge scan:
       bridgeCandidates({ fullScan: true }) = 11435, 11436 ... 11445   (11 ports, /bridge/health)
       then tagsAt('http://127.0.0.1:11434')                           (1 port, /api/tags)
     = the 12 ports. (A saved fl_bridgePort outside that range would make it 13.)
  d) After that, scheduleLoop() looks again at 8 s, then 16 s, then every 30 s for up to five
     minutes (LOOP_MAX_MS). It does a full rescan on every third tick after a miss, and stops
     once a local mind is remembered. On settings.html, panelVisible() never stops it, because
     there is no #tab-connect element on that page.
  e) The "only when you ask" words belong to local-mind-probe.js (the grandmother card). That
     card really does look only after its tap. The look on load comes from the Connect door
     above it. So the code is fine. The page just claims something that one of its two parts
     doesn't do.
  f) FreeLattice has the same mismatch: the app.html look card (#fl-look-card, data-look="0")
     says "It only knocks on well-known local doors, and only when you ask." But FreeLattice
     looks at boot today (flBridgeDetectBoot, owAutoDetect, autoDiscoverAI, Zero-Click), and
     after the boot probe it still does exactly ONE look on open (FlColdStart.start() ->
     FlConnect.bootProbe({ fullScan: true })), plus Connect's own loop while Connect is open.

2. THE CHOICE: THE WORDS MATCH THE BEHAVIOR
======================================================================
Why this way:
  - The model-choice and boot fixes need a look at open. The boot probe's whole point is one
    shared look at open, so the person sees "Ollama is ready, pick a mind" (FreeLattice F1
    voice; on the Tree, mount() becomes lookAgain({ reuse: true }) and joins that look). Making
    Connect wait for a tap would undo F1/F2 and add a second "May I look?" button on Tree
    Settings (cc-note-2026-09-06: "the door is one door").
  - Simpler for Mom: she opens Settings and sees what's there. She still chooses with one tap.
    The real line is "nothing is chosen until you tap", and both repos keep it.
  - It's the smallest honest change: words only. fl-connect.js isn't touched, so there's no
    byte-identical risk and no clash with the boot probe's fl-connect hunks.
  - The looks are local only: 127.0.0.1, Bridge health plus Ollama tags (and LM Studio after
    the boot probe). Nothing goes over the network, no files are read, and nothing is uploaded.
    So saying it plainly costs no trust.
Not chosen (written down so review is quick): an askFirst option on FlConnect.mount(el, opts)
that renders Connect quiet until Look again is tapped, used only by the Tree's two callers. It
would touch the exact line the boot probe rewrites (mount's lookAgain({ reuse: true })) and both
repos' shared file. If Kirk ever wants the Tree to be tap-only, that's the path, as a separate
v0.2.

New words:
  Tree card (PRIMARY_SOFT):  "May I look? Tap to look again, any time. Same door."
  Tree card (honesty line):  "We only look at well-known doors on this computer. Opening Settings
                              starts a short look (a few minutes at most), and Find local minds
                              looks again when you tap. Nothing is chosen until you tap a mind.
                              We never look through this computer's files. We never upload.
                              Quality of the sky is later."
  FreeLattice look card:     "FreeLattice can look for minds already living on this computer. It
                              only knocks on this computer's own well-known doors. It looks once
                              when FreeLattice opens, while Connect is open, and whenever you ask.
                              Nothing is chosen until you tap a mind."
  Tree code-settings.html:   a Newer note under "Nothing looks, chooses, or persists until that
                              tap", keeping the old words above it.
Not changed: the Gathering's "Find local minds" aria-label ("May I look — only when you ask")
stays. That button really does look only on tap, and the Gathering never mounts Connect.
