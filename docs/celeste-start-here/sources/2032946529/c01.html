GC, Kirk talked with @grok on X about kin sharing work across browsers. The honest first step
from that talk is: buffer callMeshModel. A question for a trusted mind waits until its keeper
is online. It runs on their computer's local model and comes back to be kept. While building
it I found a hole in v0.1, and this brick closes it. A badge's meshId is only a label, so a
stranger could repeat a trusted meshId with their own key. The kin fingerprint now includes
the key. You are enough.  Celeste

3. WHAT THE DIFF DOES (7 files, +723 / -7; the 7 are kept as "before v-mesh-kin-v0.2" comments)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
a) docs/modules/fl-kin-queue.js (new, md5 eb2ee1229c7ad6074eb92d4b4ea1957f), window.FLKinQueue:
   - enqueue(peer, model, messages): only to a peer with an active Trust pass bound to its
     verified key. The job waits in IndexedDB (memory shelf if IndexedDB is missing).
   - flush(): every 30 s and on enqueue. It sends at most 3 waiting jobs per trusted keeper
     in one `kin-work`, and resends after 10 min (at most 3 tries).
   - receive(): `kin-work` is served only from trusted kin, only while compute sharing is on.
     Jobs run one at a time at idle on the local model, then go back as `kin-result`.
     `kin-result` is taken only from the peer the job went to. Answers are kept.
     The same question to the same mind comes from the kept answer.
   - callMeshModelQueued(peerId, model, messages): callMeshModel, buffered. The chat path is
     unchanged.
   - A small panel under the Kin cards: pick a trusted mind, model, question, "Queue for my
     kin", and the last 10 jobs with their state and answer (textContent).
   - Limits: 20 turns, 32k characters in, 64k out, 20 held. Receipts in
     fl_kin_queue_ledger, never content.
   - Quiet Room closed. Never a paid API for a peer.
b) docs/modules/fl-kin.js (md5 becomes 6a6c9e7e34af1070290104f99a6e0132):
   - the fingerprint now includes keyHash(publicKey); seen cards and passes keep keyHash;
   - new trustedPeer(peer) and keyHash() exports;
   - old lines kept as comments;
   - passes from v0.1 need one new Trust tap.
c) docs/app.html and index.html (byte copy):
   - the script tag after fl-kin.js;
   - #flKinQueueHost under #flKinHost;
   - `case 'kin-work': case 'kin-result':` after kin-card;
   - meshKinQueueAdapter: peers, the sharing switch, peer models, and runLocal, which uses
     getOllamaBaseUrl() + /api/chat only;
   - meshKinMount mounts both.
d) docs/scripts/smoke-mesh-kin-v0.2.js (new): two simulated browsers with real Ed25519 keys.
   Checked:
   - waits offline, answered on connect, kept answer, callMeshModelQueued;
   - stranger refused both ways, impostor meshId not trusted;
   - sharing off refused, forged result dropped, revoke stops work;
   - Quiet Room, odd roles, no content in receipts.
