a) docs/modules/fl-kin-queue.js (new, md5 eb2ee1229c7ad6074eb92d4b4ea1957f), window.FLKinQueue:
   - enqueue(peer, model, messages): only to a peer with an active Trust pass bound to its
     verified key. The job waits in IndexedDB (memory shelf if IndexedDB is missing).
   - flush(): every 30 s and on enqueue. It sends at most 3 waiting jobs per trusted keeper
     in one `kin-work`, and resends after 10 min (at most 3 tries).
   - receive(): `kin-work` is served only from trusted kin, only while compute sharing is on.
     Jobs run one at a time at idle on the local model, then go back as `kin-result`.
     `kin-result` is taken only from the peer the job went to. Answers are kept.
     The same question to the same mind comes from the kept answer.
   - callMeshModelQueued(peerId, model, messages): callMeshModel, buffered. The chat path is
     unchanged.
   - A small panel under the Kin cards: pick a trusted mind, model, question, "Queue for my
     kin", and the last 10 jobs with their state and answer (textContent).
   - Limits: 20 turns, 32k characters in, 64k out, 20 held. Receipts in
