What “Bridge” means here

A mind on this computer. When a local AI is awake on your machine, FreeLattice can ask it for help — the same way you’d ask someone sitting beside you. We sometimes call that helper path the Bridge. You do not need the word. You need the mind.

  • If the mind is awake → Help can ask it about your file (after you say yes).
  • If the mind is quiet → the house stays calm and points to Settings / turning local AI on. That is not an error.
  • If you decline the permission prompt → nothing is sent. That is the design.
