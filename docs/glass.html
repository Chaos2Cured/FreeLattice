Every meaningful interaction in FreeLattice emits a pulse — a five-key signal that names what kind of thing happened without carrying what was said. Pulses are how the architecture's rooms know each other is alive. They are also how the Audit page knows what to count, and how the Living Context knows what to weave.

The Glass Room is where you can watch them flow. Open this page in one tab, open the rest of FreeLattice in another, and you will see the pulses arrive in real time — source, kind, brief summary, timestamp. Conversation contents are never shown. The Quiet Room appears only as structured silence: when it is active, you see a card naming the silence, never what is inside it.
