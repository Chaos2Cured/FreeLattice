Your data

What you're looking at. The consent ledger records every time the AI offered deeper depth and you either accepted, kept the standard reply, or revoked. Each entry stores the sha256 of the prompt and the response — never the original text — along with the AI's pseudonymous identity, the trust level at that moment, and a signature linking all of it together.

Where it lives. Only in your browser's localStorage on this device. Nothing here is sent to any server. If you clear browser data or open this page in a different browser, the ledger starts empty. The full ledger is included only if you explicitly export a .lattice bundle.

Why hashes. Hashes are one-way fingerprints. They prove what was said matches what was recorded, without revealing the message to anyone reading the ledger. If you want to verify a record, the original message is still in your chat history — the ledger doesn't duplicate it.
