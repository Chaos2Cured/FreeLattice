Known Issue: Browser Caching

GitHub Pages uses Fastly CDN. After a push, the CDN may serve stale content for up to 10 minutes. If you see "V7.0" instead of "V7.2-FINAL", try:

  • Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R)
  • Cache-buster URL: freelattice.com/simulation-v3?v=72final
  • Wait 10 minutes and try again
