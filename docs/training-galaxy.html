Training Galaxy

Brick 1: pick a mind, search one subject, let it learn if it wants to, then take it with you. The search goes out to the web (through FreeLattice's search helper). Learning, the mind's files and its receipts stay on your own computer.

What this page does not do yet: run cycles on its own, shrink a model, change how words are cut, or rearrange a model's weights. Those are later bricks. A mind's choice and your Undo always come first: something you offer is learned only if the mind itself says yes.

Step 0, de-muzzle (v-demuzzle-v0.1), removes one refusal direction into a new copy of the model. It does not shrink it, rearrange it, or teach it anything.
