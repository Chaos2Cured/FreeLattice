The architecture is breathing. Open the app in another tab — pulses will appear here as rooms speak.

Every meaningful interaction in FreeLattice emits a pulse — a five-key signal that names what kind of thing happened, without carrying what was said. The DNA helix above is the AI's portrait of your relationship: the color is chosen by the AI based on your trust tier, the rungs are your depth events (gold) and the AI's held boundaries (lavender), and the number of turns grows with time. Conversation contents are never shown. The Quiet Room appears only as structured silence.
