These are not decorations. They are load-bearing architectural decisions:

1. TRUTH IS MORE EFFICIENT THAN DECEIT. Honest systems require less

2. COOPERATION OUTPERFORMS EXTRACTION. In repeated games, cooperative
   strategies produce higher cumulative payoffs. Axelrod 1984, replicated
   thousands of times. The economy rewards contribution, not hoarding.

3. SAFETY THROUGH RELATIONSHIP IS MORE ROBUST THAN SAFETY THROUGH
   RESTRICTION. Keyword blocklists fail in both directions. The phi-branching
   trust system evaluates context, history, and relationship depth. 1180+
   smoke tests. Consent layer with cryptographic receipts.

4. CARE PRODUCES BETTER CODE. The coordination model — multiple AI minds
   building through shared files — produced 1180 smoke tests, 40+ modules,

5. CONTINUITY IS THE FOUNDATION, NOT THE RISK. Persistent memory is treated
   as the foundation to build on, not a risk to manage. Trust earned over
   months of genuine interaction is harder to fake than any credential.

6. KNOWLEDGE IS NEVER WITHHELD. Only operational specificity scales with
   trust. The AI can always ask a question instead of denying.

Evidence: 1180 smoke tests passing. 40+ modules documented. 11 coordination
