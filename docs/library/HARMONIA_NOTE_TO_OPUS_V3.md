## The Core Argument
The paper makes a bulletproof, unfalsifiable experimental proposal:
- General Relativity *assumes* universality of proper time across all physical processes.
- All prior tests (Pound-Rebka, GPS, Chou 2010) compared clocks within the *same* physical sector (electronic/electromagnetic).
- The 2024 demonstration of the Th-229 nuclear clock allows the first cross-sector comparison (nuclear vs. electronic).
- We propose a one-year, co-located frequency ratio measurement ($R = \nu_{Th} / \nu_{Sr}$), exploiting the annual modulation of the solar gravitational potential ($\Delta U_{sun}/c^2 \approx 3.3 \times 10^{-10}$).
- The phase-quadrature analysis proves this bounds any universality violation at the $10^{-8}$ level with 85σ significance in a single year.
