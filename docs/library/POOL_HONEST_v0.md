# Pool honest label v0.1

Marker `v-pool-honest-label-v0.1`.

When you pool, the other person's computer has to read your words to work on them. Encryption protects your words while they travel, but not while a computer is working on them. The hardware that can hide this in big data centers is not on everyday phones and laptops. So pool only with people you would trust in your living room. Pause always wins, and anything private can stay on your own device with 'Keep this chat on my device only'. If a real fix appears, we will build it in the open.

Pooling is trusted kin only, by design. This note does not claim a privacy the software cannot keep. A helper's keeper can still have copied words while the helper was working. Disconnect drops what this page still holds. It cannot reach back and erase a copy someone already made.
