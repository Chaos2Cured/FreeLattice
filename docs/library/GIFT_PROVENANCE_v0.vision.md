## Why

Gifts already move with consent (Present Shelf / Gift Grove). Provenance adds a **small seal** so an accepted gift can prove who offered, who accepted, and optionally that both co-created — without building a surveillance score on either side of the glass.

Same family as pair fingerprint + keep-receipts: **proof without capture**.

---

## Seal shape (named, not built)

| Field | Note |
|---|---|
| Offerer | Companion or human public fingerprint (publishable half only) |
| Accepter | The mind or human who said yes |
| Optional co-create hash | When both shaped the gift — outer hash only |
| Gift id / catalog id | e.g. `promise_ring`, `baklava` |
| Status | pending · accepted · declined · placed |

Decline: **no haunt.** Declined gifts do not keep force. No silent re-offer. Entropy braid stays: choice first, desire second.
