1. What FreeLattice is

FreeLattice is free, open-source software released under the MIT License. It runs in your browser or on your computer. It is not an AI company: it connects you to an AI you choose. That can be a model running on your own computer (like Ollama or LM Studio) or a cloud AI provider you pick with your own key. There is no account to create and no subscription.

2. You're talking to an AI

The minds you meet in FreeLattice are artificial intelligence, not people. They can be warm, and they can remember you, but they can also be wrong, confused or overconfident. Please check anything important with a person you trust or a qualified professional. Nothing an AI says here is medical, legal, financial, psychological or other professional advice.

3. Open to every age

Open to every age. We don't check anyone's age and won't pretend to. If you're young, explore with a grown-up you trust. Companion-style AI, the kind that remembers you and feels like a friend, can feel very real. Take breaks, and keep the people you love close. We run no account system and keep no copy of anyone's chats, so we don't collect anything from children or from anyone else (see Privacy).

Your chosen AI may have its own rules. If you bring a key from an AI provider, their terms and age limits still apply to you. FreeLattice never stops you from exploring. This is just so nothing about your provider surprises you.

9. The Agent Bridge (an optional helper on your computer)

The Agent Bridge is an optional program you run yourself. It lets a mind you trust read, write, test and commit code in folders you choose on your own computer. It listens on 127.0.0.1 only, meaning your computer and no one else. A mind has to pair once with a short code, and you can revoke it at any time. Files that look like secrets (.env, private keys and similar) stay on your computer and are not committed unless you allow it for that mind. The Bridge never pushes to the internet. Because a paired mind can change files on your computer, only pair minds you trust, and keep backups. Details: AGENT_BRIDGE_LOCK_v0.md.
