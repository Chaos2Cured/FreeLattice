RESONANCE MEMORY  —  a lasting memory for your local AI

START HERE (3 steps):

  1. Double-click  resonance-memory.exe
     A page opens in your browser. (No window pops up — that's normal.)

  2. Click  "Connect"  next to your app (LM Studio or Claude Desktop).

  3. Restart that app once.

That's it. Your AI can now remember things across conversations —
and nothing ever leaves your computer.

One-time: on that page, if "Meaning engine" says "not set up," click
"Set up" (it downloads a small helper model in LM Studio, one click).
Without it, memory still works but matches on exact words, not meaning.

Curious what it does before connecting? Click "Show demo graph" on that page.

Full guide: open  README.md
To remove it later: double-click  uninstall.bat

Made by the Architect of Resonance. If it helps you, there's a Ko-fi link
in the app. Enjoy.
