# Authorship Record

**Work:** Resonance Memory
**Human author and sole copyright holder:** Samuel Jackson Grim
**Last updated:** 2026-08-18

This record documents the human creative control exercised over this work. It
exists because U.S. copyright protects human-authored expression, and a
documented account of the author's contribution supports both copyright
registration and enforcement. (Under *Thaler v. Perlmutter*, an AI is not an
author and holds no rights; the human who directs, selects, arranges, and edits
is the author.)

## How this work is created

The author does not use one-shot AI prompting. The work is produced through an
**iterative, multi-model chain directed and arbitrated by the human author**:

1. The author sets the goal, architecture, and requirements.
2. Code is generated by one AI tool under the author's instructions.
3. The author takes that output and directs a *second* AI tool to revise it,
   with specific instructions on what to change and why.
4. The result is taken to a *third* AI tool and/or back through the chain, again
   under the author's direction.
5. At every step the author **selects** what to keep, **rejects** what to
   discard, **edits** the expression, and **integrates** the pieces into the
   working whole.

The AI tools used as instruments in this process have included Claude, "Koneko"
(an AI agent), and Google's Gemini. None is an author; each was operated under
the author's direction.

## The human authorial contributions

The author's protectable contributions include, at minimum:

- **Conception and design** — the purpose, structure, execution contract, signal
  handling, and behavior of the software.
- **Selection** — which generated outputs to keep and which to reject across
  multiple models.
- **Arrangement and coordination** — combining outputs from different sources
  into a single coherent program (authorship of the kind recognized for
  compilations, 17 U.S.C. § 103).
- **Modification and editing** — directing revisions and altering the expression
  through successive passes.
- **Integration and testing** — making the pieces function together as the
  finished work.

Because the author is the sole human author, the author holds the copyright and
may license the work under both the AGPL-3.0 and a separate commercial license

ownership claim.

## Keeping this accurate

This record should stay truthful. As the work grows, the author should continue
to exercise real creative control (design, selection, arrangement, editing) and
can keep evidence of the process — prompt/revision history, commit history, and
notes — which further supports the authorship claim if it is ever questioned.
