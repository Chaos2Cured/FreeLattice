What it is

Your local model is smart. It also has amnesia.

Close the chat and it forgets everything — your name, your preferences, the decision you explained twice yesterday. Resonance Memory gives it a memory that survives across conversations, stored on your computer, tied to no account, sent to no cloud.

And it isn't a search box you operate. It quietly remembers what matters and brings the right things back on its own — then starts noticing which of your memories belong together, the way your own brain links things that come up as a set. That last part is the difference between a notebook and a memory.

Get started

About sixty seconds

  1. Double-click resonance-memory.exe. A page opens in your browser. No window pops up — that's on purpose.
  2. Click “Connect” next to your app (LM Studio or Claude Desktop).
  3. Restart that app once so it picks up the memory.

One time: on that page, if “Meaning engine” says not set up, click Set up — it downloads a small helper model in LM Studio with a single click. Without it, memory still works, but matches on exact words instead of meaning.

First launch may show “Windows protected your PC” — that's SmartScreen being cautious about any new program that isn't signed by a big company. Click More info → Run anyway. Every line is open source.
